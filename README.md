<a id="lighting"></a>
## ☀️ Advanced Lighting

Lighting is one of the central systems in Apex Radiance. The mod expands how The Sims 3 handles lamp illumination, improving where light reaches and how it interacts with the environment.

### Outdoor lighting

- **Light across lot boundaries:** streetlights illuminate nearby lots, while lot lamps contribute light to surrounding roads and terrain.
- **Ground illumination:** grass, sidewalks, streets and lot surfaces receive smoother pools of lamp light.
- **Upper-floor lamps:** balcony and terrace lights can illuminate the ground below.
- **Architecture:** walls, foundations, roofs and other exterior surfaces receive nearby lamp light.
- **Objects and foliage:** nearby lights affect gardens, fences, furniture and other outdoor details.
- **Windows:** outward-facing window surfaces can receive light from outdoor lamps.
- **Weather and snow:** lighting corrections cover snowy surfaces and rain/snow variants; walked-on sidewalks remain visible through snow.

### Indoor lighting

- **Light between floors:** indoor lamps can illuminate through stairwells and open floors.
- **Architectural occlusion:** solid floors and walls continue to block light.
- **Doors and windows:** lighting behavior accounts for openings and enclosed spaces.
- **Rooms at night:** additional controls affect background illumination and lamp light in otherwise unlit rooms.

### Customize your lighting

Choose between **Subtle**, **Soft** and **Natural** lighting styles, or use Custom to tune the result yourself.

Adjust the brightness of the ground, roads, objects, buildings and other surfaces. Additional controls include lamp color, moonlight and background illumination.

<a id="image-effects"></a>
## 🎨 Image Effects & Post-Processing

Apex includes a collection of image effects that can be enabled independently and combined to create different visual styles.

### Color grading

The Color page organizes image adjustments into groups. Each group can be enabled or disabled independently without losing its saved settings.

| Group | Controls |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Basic** | Brightness, contrast, saturation, temperature and sharpness |
| **Tones** | Midtones, shadows, highlights and black levels |
| **Color** | Split toning, six-color mixer and vignette |
| **Detail** | Clarity, sharpness and darker corners |
| **Filters** | 25 built-in looks and effects, including Technicolor, DPX Cineon, Vintage, Cross-process, Black and White, Night Mode, LUT, Auto Exposure, Glow, Halation, Film Grain, CRT and color-blind mode |

Filters are disabled by default. Each filter has its own switch, strength and expandable controls. Right-click a filter to assign a keyboard shortcut; shortcuts are never assigned automatically.

The dedicated **LUTs** area supports PNG and 3D CUBE LUT files placed in the Apex `LUTs` folder, with adjustable strength.

Before-and-after controls let you compare your adjustments against the game's original image.

### Ambient Occlusion

Ambient Occlusion adds contact shadows beneath furniture, in corners, around buildings and wherever surfaces meet.

- Five quality levels.
- Adjustable strength and distance.
- Optional support in map view.

### Sim Occlusion

Sim Occlusion has separate controls for the body and hair. You can limit how dark the added shading becomes and enable support for compatible transparent hair.

### Depth Blur

Depth Blur softens scenery outside the area you focus on. Adjust the focus to achieve the desired effect. The effect fades out in map view.

### Edge Smoothing

Choose between **FXAA** and **SMAA** to reduce jagged edges on roofs, fences, walls and furniture. Optional sharpening can restore fine texture detail.

> **Tip:** For the best compatibility with depth-based effects, disable the game's Edge Smoothing under **Options → Graphics** and use Apex's implementation instead.

### Banding Fix

Banding Fix helps smooth visible steps in gradual lighting and shadow transitions, with an optional setting for smoother sky gradients.

<a id="looks-and-shaders"></a>
## 🧩 ReShade & GShade Looks

<badge>Introduced in 4.0.0 · Beta</badge>

Apex Radiance can run compatible ReShade and GShade presets directly inside its own rendering pipeline. You can use existing `.ini` looks without installing the original ReShade or GShade injector separately.

Choose a look, adjust its overall strength, reorder and tune its shaders, and save the result as your own look.

### What is included?

- **ReShade and GShade presets:** import compatible `.ini` preset files.
- **Built-in shaders:** 17 shader implementations are available directly in Apex.
- **Your own shaders:** add compatible `.fx` and `.fxh` files to the Apex Shaders folder.
- **Shader packs:** browse and download packs from ReShade's official shader list.
- **Shader parameters:** adjust supported switches, choices, colors, vectors and numeric values through the Apex interface.
- **Look management:** switch, reorder, enable or disable individual shaders and save custom looks.
- **Preset integration:** include a look and selected shader files when exporting a preset.

Only shaders that can run with The Sims 3's Direct3D 9 capabilities are listed as usable. Unsupported shaders remain identifiable in the interface with an explanation of what is missing or incompatible.

### Shader packs

The Packs tab provides access to shader packs from their authors' GitHub repositories. Apex downloads a pack only when you choose to do so.

- Browse and search available packs.
- See which packs are installed and which shaders they provide.
- Download missing packs required by an imported look.
- Update or remove downloaded packs.
- Keep your own shader files separate from downloaded packs.

Each pack remains subject to its author's license. Apex does not silently download shader packs in the background.

### Important compatibility notes

- The Shaders page is marked **Beta**.
- Not every ReShade or GShade shader is compatible with Direct3D 9 or The Sims 3.
- Shaders that duplicate an Apex effect can produce excessive or conflicting results. Apex identifies several common overlaps and can offer to disable the corresponding Apex effect.
- Do not run another ReShade or GShade injector alongside Apex when both are applying the same effects.

## ⚡ Performance & Engine Optimizations

Apex includes optional engine-level optimizations designed to reduce repeated work, improve processing efficiency and make some operations less disruptive during gameplay.

Performance settings are grouped by the type of operation they affect.

| Category | Examples |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Camera & Lighting** | Faster room lighting, paced lot-lighting updates while moving the camera, delayed wall-shading updates and incremental object processing |
| **Files & Objects** | Faster game-file lookups, remembered missing files, faster file lists and object lookups |
| **Textures & Sims** | Faster texture compression, multithreaded texture compression, faster cache compression and faster Sim building |
| **Memory Handling** | Improved memory handling and Room to Save |
| **Game & Scripts** | Lighter window updates and faster selected script operations |

Each setting can be enabled or disabled independently. Keep the options that help your system and disable any that do not.

Experimental options are clearly marked. **Lot Streaming** is disabled by default and provides additional controls for how distant lots remain detailed and appear while moving through the world.

> **Note:** Performance features are optional and some are experimental. If you encounter instability, disable the optimizations individually to identify the cause. Keep backups of your saves.

<a id="memory"></a>
## 🧠 Memory Management

The Sims 3 is a 32-bit application, which means it has a limited address space regardless of how much physical RAM your computer has.

Apex includes improvements intended to make better use of available memory and reduce unnecessary pressure during longer sessions.

### Room to Save

**Room to Save** helps prepare memory for saving when the game is running low on available memory. It reserves space for the save operation and releases eligible idle resources where possible.

This is designed to make saving more reliable under memory pressure. It does not guarantee that Error 12 or other save failures will never occur.

### WRAP — Restart Host

Apex also includes an experimental companion executable named `ts3wrap.exe`.

**Restart Host** can restart The Sims 3 into the most recently saved world, giving the game a fresh 32-bit address space without requiring the player to manually relaunch it.

When enabled, the feature can offer to restart the game after the player exits normally following a save. The player must explicitly confirm the restart.

1. Save your current progress.
2. Exit the game normally.
3. Confirm the restart prompt.
4. Continue in the most recently saved world with a fresh address space.

> **Note:** Restart Host is experimental and disabled by default. It requires Room to Save and the companion executable. It does not automatically terminate the game or guarantee protection against crashes and memory-related issues.

<a id="rtx-remix-bridge"></a>
## 🌉 RTX Remix Bridge — 64-bit Rendering

<div align="center">
  <p>
    <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/64--BIT-RENDERER-7B61FF?style=for-the-badge" alt="64-bit renderer">
    <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/VULKAN-BACKEND-646CFF?style=for-the-badge" alt="Vulkan backend">
  </p>
  <h3>Move the renderer out of the game's 32-bit address space.</h3>
</div>

The **RTX Remix Bridge** allows The Sims 3 to use a separate 64-bit rendering process. The game itself remains a 32-bit application; the bridge moves the DXVK renderer into its own 64-bit process, separating much of the renderer's memory usage from the game's limited address space.

In tested configurations, this can leave approximately **600–800 MB more address space available to the game**, depending on the system and configuration.

### How it works

<table width="100%">
  <tr>
    <td align="center" width="33%" valign="top">
      <h2>32-bit</h2>
      <strong>The Sims 3</strong><br>
      <sub>Game simulation and assets</sub>
    </td>
    <td align="center" width="33%" valign="middle">
      <h2>⇄</h2>
      <strong>Bridge</strong><br>
      <sub>Communication between processes</sub>
    </td>
    <td align="center" width="33%" valign="top">
      <h2>64-bit</h2>
      <strong>DXVK Renderer</strong><br>
      <sub>Vulkan rendering process</sub>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

1. The Sims 3 runs as its normal 32-bit process.
2. The bridge communicates with a separate 64-bit renderer process.
3. DXVK handles Direct3D 9 rendering through Vulkan in that separate process.
4. Renderer allocations are moved outside the game's 32-bit address space.

### What you should know

- It does **not** turn The Sims 3 into a 64-bit game.
- It does **not** enable ray tracing.
- It does **not** require an NVIDIA RTX graphics card.
- It requires a compatible Vulkan driver and graphics hardware supported by DXVK.
- The image is intended to be the same as using DXVK directly.
- DXVK without the bridge remains available as an alternative.
- It requires Apex Radiance 3.0.0 or later.

### Recommended renderer

Starting with **Apex Radiance Setup 1.1.7**, the RTX Remix Bridge is selected by default for a first installation. If the graphics driver cannot run the bridge, Setup selects DXVK instead.

When updating an existing installation, Setup keeps the renderer already selected.

Apex detects the bridge automatically. You can check its status under **Settings → Compatibility**, where it appears as *RTX Remix bridge: Installed*.

### Manual installation

1. Close the game.
2. Download the manual-install archive from the [latest release](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/latest).
3. Back up or remove an existing `d3d9.dll` from `Game\Bin` if it belongs to another DXVK installation.
4. Copy the contents of the `RTX Remix bridge` folder into `Game\Bin`, keeping the `.trex` folder.
5. For the EA app version, change `client.targetProcess = TS3W.exe` to `client.targetProcess = TS3.exe` in both `bridge.conf` files.

The bridge can be removed by deleting its files and restoring any previous `d3d9.dll`. Bridge logs are written to `rtx-remix\logs`; attach them with the Apex log when reporting a problem.

Apex stores settings, profiles, LUTs, looks, shaders, logs and captures in:

```text
Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 3\Apex Radiance\
```

The main settings file is `ApexRadiance.toml`. Saved presets remain in `Profiles`, color tables in `LUTs`, looks in `Looks` and player shader files in `Shaders`.

<a id="compatibility"></a>
## 🔧 Compatibility

- **Sims3SettingsSetter:** works alongside Apex. Apex detects its active Split-Level Lighting Fix and avoids applying the same fix twice.
- **Sims 3 Performance Patch:** can run alongside Apex; the mods target different game operations.
- **DXVK:** supported. Results can vary between graphics backends; include the backend in a bug report.
- **RTX Remix Bridge:** supported from Apex Radiance 3.0.0.
- **Steam Deck and Proton:** Apex includes platform detection and a Performance profile recommendation. The Steam Deck and Proton paths are documented, but are not validated in-game to the same extent as Windows.

Some combinations of graphics injectors or shader replacements may conflict. Avoid running multiple tools that apply the same rendering effects.

## 🐛 Reporting a Problem

Apex includes integrated diagnostics to make bug reports more useful.

Open **System → Report a problem** in the Apex menu to:

- Save a report containing logs and settings.
- Record a short reproduction session.
- Capture lighting at a specific point.
- Save a lighting snapshot.
- Include a screenshot with Apex effects.
- Add a title and description to a capture.

Captures are stored in dated folders under:

```text
Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 3\Apex Radiance\Captures\
```

Zip the folder for the issue and attach it to a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/issues) or post it in the mod's Bugs tab on Nexus Mods.

When reporting a problem, include what happened, how to reproduce it, your game version, whether you use DXVK or the RTX Remix Bridge, and any other graphics mods that are installed.

Nothing is uploaded automatically. You choose what to share.
