**Your game now has a home: Apex Launcher**

Open Apex Launcher and your neighbourhood is waiting on the first screen: your last household, a Play button, and every picture you took of them. Mods, saves, worlds and updates all sit one click away, and nothing needs administrator rights unless a change does.

## Home
- Play starts the game with your updates already in
- Your newest save, your packs and what is installed at a glance
- The launcher waits in the notification area while you play

## Health
- A crystal shows how your game is doing, with each check by area
- Safe fixes apply on their own, with a copy of every file kept
- Restore points and your last session, one tab away

## Mods
- Every mod with its kind, folder and an on and off switch
- Tags, notes, merge and split, and a finder for the mod behind a problem

## Worlds and Saves
- The worlds you can start a new game in, by pack
- Each save with its household photo, Play this save and a backup

## Gallery
- Your pictures and videos from wherever Apex saves them
- Albums by day or household, favourites, captions and a search
- A scrapbook board with frames, notes, stickers and albums as piles
- Undo and redo any change with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y

## Look
- Light or dark and your accent colour, as Windows is set

## Fixes
- Turning mods on and off never leaves two in one place
- Merging, splitting and moving mods keep every file safe
- A half-typed name stays when the window changes size
- Smooth Motion's card warns that recording with the NVIDIA app freezes the game

## Install
**Recommended: Apex Launcher.** Download `ApexLauncher.exe` below, run it, pick your game and click Install. It adds itself to the Start menu and the desktop. Already using Apex Radiance Setup? It offers the update on its start page.

**Manual:** download `Apex-Radiance-4.1.0-Manual-install.zip`, extract it and follow `How to install.txt`: an ASI loader in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin`, then `ApexRadiance.asi` (and optionally `ts3wrap.exe` and the RTX Remix bridge folder) into the same folder. To update, replace `ApexRadiance.asi`.
