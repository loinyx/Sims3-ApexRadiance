# Apex Radiance 4.0.4

2026-11-18 · [v4.0.4](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v4.0.4) · ApexRadianceSetup.exe, Apex-Radiance-4.0.4-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**ReShade effects as ReShade runs them, and a Shaders page that keeps your changes.** Players' ReShade effects give the
same picture as ReShade 6.7.3, and the Shaders page no longer crashes the game or drops a change. Three places where
indoor lamp light passed through walls are fixed.

## New features

- **Look list with favourites, rename and delete.** A deleted look leaves the list at once and reaches the Recycle Bin
  when its Undo toast is gone ([Looks](../features/looks.md)).
- **Trash button on every shader row.** A removed row stays in place with Restore until the look is saved; a shader
  switched off stays listed and is saved off (`ApexOff=`).
- **True-size furniture shadows.** Night Lights > Rooms at Night, on by default with Lamp updates: Instant (GPU): the
  furniture's light blockers keep their real size, their edges as soft as the lamp seen past them; off keeps the game's
  band, 0.15 m inside to 0.35 m outside each blocker ([GPU room lamps](../features/night-lighting/gpu-room-lamps.md)).

## Changes

- **Effects run as in ReShade 6.7.3.** Compared with ReShade 6.7.3 (its ReShade32.dll on the same device) over 12
  shader packs: 219 of 219 techniques identical to the bit with no depth, 217 of 219 with a scene depth bound.
  - Depth shaders (MXAO, SMAA, fog, depth of field) read the picture after the game's colour curve; shaders listed
    before a depth shader run before it.
  - Textures with the same name are shared between effects; key, mouse, mouse wheel, `overlay_open`, timer and
    frametime sources; `__VENDOR__` and `__DEVICE__` from the graphics card.
  - Render target mips are rebuilt after each pass; images are resized with ReShade's stb_image_resize2; COLOR is
    copied only before a pass that reads it.
  - A ReShade.ini's global definitions are read; includes are found in every subfolder; a missing texture file no
    longer refuses an effect.
- **Saving a look.** The Look row marks unsaved changes and its Reset returns to the saved look; picking another look
  or importing one asks to save, discard or cancel; Save to this look keeps a `.bak`, and an Apex or GShade look is
  saved into My looks.
- **Shader rows.** Choice settings are dropdowns, value rows line up, shader descriptions are translated; quality
  settings that cannot compile on the player's PC are not offered, and a setting edited in the session that breaks a
  shader is put back to the last one that ran.

## Fixes

- **Switching off a shader added in the menu no longer closes the game.** The run list was rebuilt while its row was
  still drawn; no row rebuilds it now, and the pack list is rebuilt only at the start of a frame.
- **A shader's values and quality settings are saved with the look.**
- **Indoor lamps no longer light the outer face of walls beside windows and doors.** A wall texel whose lamp is behind
  its plane gives 0, in the room solve and in room 0's GPU draw (present since 2.13.0).
- **A room's lamps no longer light the back of a wall from the room behind it.** The behind-face rule applies to every
  physical GPU draw.
- **A low outdoor lamp no longer leaves a coloured strip on the floor just inside a wall.**

## Known limitations

- MXAO and RadiantGI differ from ReShade by at most 2 levels on under 0.01% of pixels, along one depth edge.
- Textures shared through ReShade's `pooled` keyword are not shared.
- Favourites and pending deletes are stored in the Looks table, so they are also written into profiles and exported
  presets.
