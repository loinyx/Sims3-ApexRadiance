# Apex Radiance 2.18.0

[v2.18.0](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.18.0) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

**Live lamp edits with far less CPU work, and a reorganized menu.** Moving, adding or removing a lamp or a fence now
updates the light on walls, floors, furniture and the ground live, and the costliest parts of a drag dropped by up to
97%. The Lighting page has four tabs, and every page groups its options instead of hiding them under Advanced.

## New features

- **Lighting tabs:** the Lighting page is split into Overview, Outside, Inside & floors and Game fixes. Overview sums up
  every lighting area and its options on one page; the game-bug fixes have their own tab and stay on by default, so the
  other tabs keep only the choices that change the look.
- **Ground light:** one slider for lamp light on grass, roads, sidewalks, lots, patios and snow replaces Ground
  brightness, Roads and sidewalks, Street lamp brightness and Lot lamp brightness. Configurations, profiles and looks
  saved with the four controls are migrated.
- **Applied chip on presets:** a preset shows Applied while the current setup still matches it.
- **Light through mesh fences:** chain-link fences, lattices and railings let lamp light through their gaps onto the
  ground, in the share their texture leaves open; brick and other solid garden walls still block it.

## Changes

- **Grouped cards:** cards on every page use named groups instead of Advanced folds.
- **Dragged lamps on the GPU:** a dragged lot lamp's ground light is drawn on the GPU with the bake's own law. Terrain
  work on the CPU during a drag fell from 2155 ms to 94 ms (127 to 7 terrain relights; 97% less).
- **Room light during a drag:** lots whose floor maps the game keeps smaller than its solve take the fast GPU path: room
  light work while dragging fell from 5.0 to 0.6 ms a frame.
- **Furniture, doors and windows:** they follow a picked-up lamp from its first move, instead of after 4 to 22 s.
- **Every story at once:** a new or picked-up lamp lights every story at once, and the walls of every story are relit in
  the same frame (before, a round could spread over 5 to 8 frames and the seam between stories flickered).
- **Floor shadow mask:** 2.5 times faster (1.6 to 0.65 ms a round), with the same result.
- **Drag sync:** the lot and the grass beside it, also past the lot edge, follow every step of a lamp drag.
- **Lamp cones on the lawn:** spotlights and shaded lamps light the ground through their cones in the terrain bake.
- **Fence shadows:** they follow added or removed pieces in about 0.25 s (a removed piece kept its shadow up to 20 s);
  the shade's inputs are checked every 100 ms while fences or a dragged lamp change.

## Fixes

- Wet roads no longer turn grey at night in the rain: the road's fixed sky reflection follows the daylight.
- Patios and floors keep the right lamp light in rain and snow, in all 104 weather floor variants.
- Snow on objects and outdoor objects in bad weather take lamp light.
- Patios no longer count each lamp twice: they match the grass, sidewalks and roads beside them.
- Openings in a fence let light through: fence posts at an opening's end and lamps dragged through it no longer close it.
- Lamp light no longer stays stuck on the ground after a lamp is dropped.
