# Apex Radiance 4.0.11

2026-11-19 · [v4.0.11](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v4.0.11) · ApexRadianceSetup.exe, Apex-Radiance-4.0.11-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**System > Display with Turbo Simulation Engine.** Turbo makes a throwaway graphics device; when it came right after
the game's, opening the Display page crashed the game, and 4.0.10 had covered only a device made later. Display now
works whenever it comes, and under the RTX Remix bridge Turbo's early request no longer becomes a second device.

## Fixes

- **Crash opening System > Display (again in 4.0.10).** A player's 4.0.10 log had Turbo's device 5 ms after the game's,
  before its first frame, where 4.0.10's check (the game's device, known at its first `EndScene`) could not see it.
  Display now keeps its own reference on the `IDirect3D9` it holds, remembers each device's request, and at the game's
  first frame takes the game device's `IDirect3D9`, adapter, window, parameters and own request back
  (`Screen::AdoptGameDevice`); after that frame other devices' `CreateDevice` and `Reset` do not reach Display. With the
  game's device alone nothing changes ([Display](../features/display.md)).
- **RTX Remix bridge: Turbo's request before the game's first frame.** It now gets the game's device as after that
  frame (the last device made with a size of its own, held until then) instead of a real second device
  ([RTX Remix bridge](../features/rtx-remix-bridge.md)).

## Testing

In-game runner, Turbo Simulation Engine V3.4 and `tools/second_device_probe` (Turbo's request before and after the
first frame), native Direct3D 9 and the bridge, with and without either: every start passed
([validation](../validation/display.md#another-mods-device-4011)).

## Known limitations

- The Display page itself was not opened in those runs (the runner cannot open it); the same resolution list from the
  held `IDirect3D9` ran at the first frame. Turbo's Turbo Patch and RAM options were not exercised (off without its
  game packages).
