# Apex Radiance 2.15.4

2026-10-24 · [v2.15.4](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.15.4) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

Continuous lamp light across gables and upper trim, balanced architectural objects, and shadows that follow short pillars and solid floors beneath lamps.

## New features

- **Restart lighting:** rebuild temporary light maps and refresh loaded stories and object lighting without changing settings. Unavailable GPU maps retain native lighting with an explicit partial status.

## Fixes

- **Gables and upper trim:** adjacent gable sections use one consistent lighting reference. Elevated trim no longer takes the ground's lamp pool; elevated instanced pieces use the same architectural gain as nearby objects.
- **Pillars and stairs:** short pillars block light up to their drawn top instead of a full story. A complete floor tile immediately beneath a lamp casts a soft shadow on instanced stairs and railings, separately for each lamp. Nearby foundation geometry no longer shortens a pillar's blocker.
- **Snowy lot edges:** snow and melting-snow passes use the dry lot's soft transition to terrain lighting. Snow draws also trigger the local terrain update when a lot becomes visible.
- **Scene updates:** effects and animal fur receive updates required before drawing even after the ordinary object-update budget is reached.
- **Outdoor objects:** loose architectural objects follow the walls' lamp gain with Realistic lamp reach. Gates in fence gaps retain the ground fence scale.

## Known limitations

- The standard release build has not yet been checked in game; its lighting and menu footer remain unverified.
- The per-lamp floor shadow covers verified complete tiles beneath emitters, not arbitrary object geometry or open stair treads.
- A synthetic high-intensity fence-lamp comparison differs from the wall-law reference by up to 179/255; its impact in a game scene is unverified. The ordinary-range case matches within 1/255.
- No general FPS or RAM improvement is established for this release. Scene updates still scan the remaining queue and run mandatory drawing updates after the ordinary budget.
- Intermittent loading after leaving Build/Buy for Edit Town remains under investigation; this release does not claim to fix it.
