# Apex Radiance 4.0.3

2026-11-16 · [v4.0.3](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v4.0.3) · ApexRadianceSetup.exe, Apex-Radiance-4.0.3-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**One-click frame generation in Setup.** Apex Radiance Setup 1.2.0 turns on NVIDIA Smooth Motion (RTX 40 and 50 series)
or AMD Fluid Motion Frames (cards whose driver supports it) with one switch. It also sets Apex's frame limit right
there, with 60 to 90 fps suggested.

## Changes

- **NVIDIA Smooth Motion switch.** Setup creates a driver profile, *Apex Radiance - The Sims 3*, that holds
  `NvRemixBridge.exe` and both editions' game exes, and turns Smooth Motion on in it; off deletes that profile.
  - It needs an RTX 40 or 50 series card, driver 571.86 or newer, and the RTX Remix bridge.
  - A profile of the player's own that already holds those exes is left alone.
  - The NVIDIA app lists the bridge as not compatible, but the forced profile works: the maintainer checked it on an
    RTX 40 series card with NVIDIA Profile Inspector ([Apex Radiance Setup](../features/installer.md)).
- **AMD Fluid Motion Frames switch.** Through AMD's ADLX. AFMF is one setting for the whole card, so the row says it
  applies to every game. Setup records that it turned AFMF on, and only then turns it off again.
- **Apex frame limit in Setup.** A picker beside frame generation: off, 30, 60, 75, 90, 120, 144, 165, 240 or a
  custom value, with 60, 75 and 90 marked as recommended.
  - Turning frame generation on sets 60 only when there is no limit; any limit the player chose stays.
  - Each change has its own restore point.
- **Maintenance.** Removing the RTX Remix bridge or Apex Radiance, switching away from the bridge, or uninstalling
  everything turns frame generation off, after the files are removed. A driver error never fails the removal.
- **A cleaner game list.** The chosen game shows by its card's accent outline, with no second box around it.
- **Smooth Motion in the README.** The README explains how to turn Smooth Motion on by hand with NVIDIA Profile
  Inspector, for players who don't use Setup.

## Fixes

None in the mod itself; `ApexRadiance.asi` changes only its version and its What's new.

## Known limitations

- The real driver writes are not verified: neither Setup's NVIDIA profile write nor its AMD AFMF write ran on a real
  card. The read paths ran on a real NVIDIA driver, and on the AMD driver of an integrated GPU, which reports AFMF as
  unsupported.
- AFMF is untested on an RX 6000-series or newer card, and untested under the RTX Remix bridge.
- The EA app 1.69 handling is covered by tests on a fake game, not on a real EA app install.

## Upgrade notes

Setup 1.2.0 offers itself to players with an older Setup. No settings change in the mod.
