# Apex Radiance 2.9.1

Published 2026-10-07 · tag [`v2.9.1`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.9.1) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Sold lamps stop lighting walls; exterior wall light is softer.

## Changes

- **Wall brightness:** lamp light on outside walls is softer: Soft 30%, Subtle 27%, Natural 56%. Pick a lighting style or reset to get the new values.
- **Smoother lamp edits:** less work for the wall lighting while you place and move lamps.
- **Load times in the log:** the log now records how long each loading phase takes, to help with slow-load reports.

## Fixes

- **Sold or deleted lamps:** outside walls (diagonal ones too) and decks no longer keep the light of a lamp you sold or deleted in Build mode; Undo and Redo update the walls as well.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
