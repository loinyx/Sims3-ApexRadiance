# Apex Radiance 2.16.3

[v2.16.3](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.16.3) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

A small hotfix for dark squares on lit floors under furniture.

## Fixes

- **Night Lights:** a lit floor no longer shows dark squares one tile wide under a table, a plant or a floor lamp's foot
  beside a bright lamp (*Smooth light maps*). Rooms already lit before the update show them until they light again.
