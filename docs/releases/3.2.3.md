# Apex Radiance 3.2.3

2026-11-14 · [v3.2.3](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v3.2.3) · ApexRadianceSetup.exe, Apex-Radiance-3.2.3-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**No blue flashes on loading screens under the RTX Remix bridge.** On a hybrid-graphics laptop under the bridge, loading
and save screens flashed whole frames of solid blue; they now show as in the game.

## Changes

- **Display's Turn off G-SYNC is removed.** G-SYNC follows the NVIDIA driver's own settings again. For players who had the
  switch on, the first start of 3.2.3 puts back what it changed in the NVIDIA profiles of the game and of the RTX Remix
  bridge's server, then forgets it ([Display](../features/display.md)).

## Fixes

- **Solid blue frames on loading and save screens under the RTX Remix bridge.** The High-precision scene now draws only
  frames with a 3D scene (4 or more depth-tested draws) into its 16-bit target; a frame without one is drawn straight into
  the back buffer. On an Intel Arc and RTX 4060 Laptop with the bridge (EA app 1.69), 93 to 116 frames of each 20 s
  recording on a loading screen were solid blue (0, 0, 255) with the High-precision scene on and none with it off; with
  the fix, loading, saving and placing a lot showed no blue with it on
  ([Banding Fix](../features/banding-fix.md#high-precision-scene)).

## Known limitations

- Why the 16-bit target gave solid blue on that laptop is not known; the fix keeps loading and save screens out of it.
- Checked on one player's laptop; the maintainer's machine never showed the blue frames.

## Upgrade notes

- `gsync_off` in `[screen]` is no longer read; `gsync_restore` is read once and removed after the restore.
