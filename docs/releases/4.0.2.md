# Apex Radiance 4.0.2

2026-11-16 · [v4.0.2](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v4.0.2) · ApexRadianceSetup.exe, Apex-Radiance-4.0.2-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**Steadier lot lighting when the camera moves, and a smoother game.** Lot grounds no longer turn dark as the camera
moves between lots, and the frame rate is higher, mostly when zoomed out with the RTX Remix bridge.

## Changes

- **Smoother frame rate.** The game reads its mouse pick back from the graphics card every other frame, and that copy
  blocked the game under the RTX Remix bridge (about 2 ms a call). It now waits until the graphics card has drawn the
  pick, at most 4 frames, so it runs about half as often: 1.35 s to 0.63 s of read-back in 10 s of a far camera pan in
  Sunset Valley. Steam 1.67.2 only.
- **Rooms with arches cost less to draw.** The floors' arch rows are kept for the frame instead of being worked out again
  for each draw of the same floor: Smooth light maps went from 45-75 to 14-20 µs a draw at night
  ([Night lighting](../features/night-lighting/README.md)).
- **The Quality profile uses High Ambient Occlusion instead of Ultra.** No built-in profile picks Ultra; the
  Performance profile, with Ambient Occlusion off, keeps High as its stored quality.
- **The low-memory state shows only in Overview.** The card over the game and its switch are removed; Overview > Game
  memory still shows Getting low.

## Fixes

- **Lot grounds no longer turn dark or flicker as the camera moves.** On some frames the game drew a lot's ground with no
  room light map and no lamp maps; each ground mesh now keeps the game's maps it was last drawn with and binds them back
  for such a draw.
- **Neighbouring lots keep their lamp light while the camera pans.** The active lot's neighbours are held while the
  camera moves, and a lot just made active stays active for 3 s, so a pan no longer rebuilds both lots' ground.
- **Snowy lots and roads are lit with WinterRoadsFix installed.** Its rewritten snowy lot and road shaders are
  recognised; the road patch takes the lowest free samplers when s15 is in use.
- **Shaders: no crash when changing ADOF's values.** A shader compiled after the look loaded kept an empty or stale list
  of values; the list is read again when its size does not match the shader.

- **Outdoor lamp light keeps every lamp after an edit.** Apex's small buffers are now 16-byte aligned: the game's light
  evaluation reads a room solve sample with an aligned SSE load, and an 8-byte aligned copy faulted (caught, so that lamp's
  outdoor update fell back). Contributed by Rodney Pantonial ([#92](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/pull/92)).

## Known limitations

- In a scripted camera jump between lots, the plaza in Sunset Valley can still show its darker light for a few seconds
  right after the jump, until the neighbour's lamp light is rebuilt.

## Upgrade notes

No settings change. Install over 4.0.1 with Apex Radiance Setup or by replacing `ApexRadiance.asi`.
