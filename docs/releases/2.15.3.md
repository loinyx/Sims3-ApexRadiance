# Apex Radiance 2.15.3

2026-10-23 · [v2.15.3](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.15.3) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

Light from windows and doors now adds to the garden lamps on the lot ground, wall and fence shadows on the ground take
away only the light they block, closed gates stop lamp light, windows let light through at their real size, trees on
lots with a basement are no longer white at night, ceiling lamps no longer light the closed room above, and the game
does less work at night.

## New features

- **Indoor light adds to the ground:** Lighting > Buildings > Rooms at Night > *Indoor light adds to the ground* (on by
  default). The light lit rooms send through their windows, doors and arches onto the lot ground and snowy lots adds to
  the garden and street lamps' light there instead of the brighter of the two, and the walls' shadow of the outdoor lamps
  leaves it whole. Patios and paths (story-0 floor tiles) keep the look of before. It needs *Indoor light through openings* and *Walls block light on floors*; outdoor floors keep the
  look of before ([light between stories](../features/night-lighting/level-light-share.md#indoor-light-on-the-ground)).

## Changes

- **Moonlight** is 0 by default and in the Performance, Balanced and Quality presets (0.277 in 2.15.1 and 2.15.2): at
  night only the lamps and the sky's glow light the scene ([night lighting](../features/night-lighting/README.md)).
- **Shadows on the ground:** a wall's or fence's shadow takes away only the light of the lamps it blocks and ends where
  its lamp's light on the ground ends. Behind a fence lit by one garden post it was darker than snow no lamp reaches and
  ran far past the post's light ([lot light pass](../features/night-lighting/lot-light-pass.md)).
- **Ground shadows show sooner:** the lot nearest the camera is shaded first, and a lot and the grass around it change in
  the same frame, with no seam at the lot's edge; only the changed part of the grass's shade is made again and uploaded.
- **Light through windows:** a window lets lamp light through at the size of its glass, read from the game's window
  lights, through a wall 10 cm deep and its glass (0.765 of the light); arches and open doors let all of it through
  ([light between stories](../features/night-lighting/level-light-share.md#openings-of-walls-light-through-doors-and-windows)).
- **Area lights** of a lot light only the side they face, so a white area light at ground height no longer lights a
  whole tree crown ([foliage](../features/night-lighting/foliage.md)).
- **Performance:** room solving with the camera still and the raised budgets while it moves are dosed by the frame
  time; the object lookups, the file lists, the scene's object map and Night Lighting's draws do less work; the features
  start sooner after the world load begins; the Instant lamp shaders come from the shader cache; and the screen effects
  share one copy of the picture (about 100 MB less video memory at 3840x2160)
  ([performance](../features/performance/README.md), [lot lighting while moving](../features/performance/lot-lighting-motion.md)).

## Fixes

- **White trees:** a tree on a lot with a basement was drawn flat and bright white all night; the game put it on its
  underground light rig, which never gathers lamps ([foliage](../features/night-lighting/foliage.md)).
- **Ceiling lamps and the story above:** a ceiling lamp of the lower story lit the closed rooms and the furniture of the
  story above it, also on a lot mixing a house on a foundation with one without
  ([light between stories](../features/night-lighting/level-light-share.md#lamps-under-a-storys-floor)).
- **Gates and fence gaps:** a closed gate, and gaps a few centimetres wide between fence pieces, let a garden post's
  light through onto the ground behind them ([lot light pass](../features/night-lighting/lot-light-pass.md)).
- **Fences and gates:** with *Realistic lamp reach* they took the walls' brightness on top of their own (30% of the
  light the ground beside them took), and a fence face turned to the lamp showed a dark band under 1 m
  ([fences](../features/night-lighting/fences.md)).
- **Coloured lamps** keep their colour near their heads on objects, fences and windows; they turned white there
  ([objects and rigs](../features/night-lighting/objects-and-rigs.md)).
- **Gables and eaves:** a gable, and a garage's eave beside a house on a foundation, showed a hard step of light where
  they start; walls drawn below their light base are lit from their drawn foot ([walls](../features/night-lighting/walls.md)).
- **Snowy lots** take the room mask and the walls' and fences' shadows, as the grass beside them does: a step showed at
  the lot border at night in winter ([snow](../features/night-lighting/snow.md)).
- **Rectangular lots:** on a lot whose light map is not square, the lot's lamp light lay up to half a metre off its lamp
  ([lot light pass](../features/night-lighting/lot-light-pass.md#lot-map-uv-alignment-klotlightvs)).
- **Loading:** a lamp the game switches while the world loads no longer counts as a player's edit and slows the first
  frames ([terrain relight](../features/night-lighting/terrain-relight.md)).

## Known limitations

- Not verified in game: the shadow law, gates, chunk updates and the nearest-lot order, the indoor light on the ground,
  window sizes and glass, gables and eaves, fences and gates' lamp scale, the hue of coloured lamps, the rectangular lot
  alignment, and the performance changes. The white trees and the ceiling lamps lighting the closed room above were
  checked in game.
- Story-0 outdoor floors (patios, paths) do not take the indoor light on the ground.
- Gates are detected only while the per-pixel object lamps draw. Any outdoor object standing in a 0.4 to 2.5 m fence gap closes it in the ground shadow, and an open gate shades as a closed one.
- The window sizes read from the game's window lights are not yet compared with the drawn glass; a window without them
  keeps the default size.
- The offline terrain lighting check keeps its two known failures, which also fail on 2.15.2-era commits.

## Upgrade notes

- Saved settings and profiles keep the moonlight value they hold (`luar`, 0.277 from 2.15.1 and 2.15.2); the new
  default 0 applies to new settings, *Reset* and the presets. To match it, set `luar = 0` in `ApexRadiance.toml` (the Moonlight row is in the developer-mode Individual options).
