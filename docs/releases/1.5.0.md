# Apex Radiance 1.5.0

Published 2026-09-29 · tag [`v1.5.0`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v1.5.0) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

More control over Night Lights: light styles, brightness per part, lamp colors and moonlight.

## New features

- **Light style:** Soft, Natural or Bright sets lamp brightness on the ground, objects, walls, roofs and ponds at once (Lighting > Lamps). Natural is the look you had before; fine-tune each part in its own tab afterwards.
- **Ground brightness and Roads and sidewalks:** new sliders in Lighting > Ground set how bright lamp light is on grass, lots and patios, and on roads and sidewalks compared with the ground around them. They change instantly.
- **Street lamp brightness and Lot lamp brightness:** set how strongly each kind of lamp lights the ground. The ground light is rebuilt once when you let go of the slider (a short pause).
- **Moonlight:** a new slider for how much the moon lights the world at night, from none to twice the game's. By day nothing changes.

## Changes

- **Lamp color changes right away:** no more reloading the save. Lot lamps can also have their own color, separate from street lamps ("Own color for lot lamps").
- **Walls:** "Lamps light walls" can now be switched off (the game's own dim walls), and wall brightness goes lower than before.
- **Updates:** "Update at dusk" is now its own switch, apart from "Lot lamps light the street", in Lighting > Ground > Updates, with the delay after dusk.
- **Pond reflections:** the Water Reflections card now says what they need: Night Lights and Depth Blur on, and the game's own Edge Smoothing off (Options > Graphics). Apex's Edge Smoothing works with them.
- The new options are translated into Portuguese, Spanish and French.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
