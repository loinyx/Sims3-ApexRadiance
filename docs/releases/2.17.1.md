# Apex Radiance 2.17.1

[v2.17.1](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.17.1) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

A lamp's light stays in its own room: floors, counters, door frames and every other object behind a wall no longer take
the next room's lamp, while doorways and arches still let it through. Street light no longer reaches counters inside the
house, and lamp light on fences and outdoor objects is worked out less often.

## Changes

- **Lamp light on fences and outdoor objects:** the lamps picked for a fence piece are kept 1024 at a time (was 256), and
  the picks of objects near lamps shining out of a house are kept too, tested on a snapshot of those lamps: in a measured
  session about 90% of the fence picks were reused.

## Fixes

- **Light through walls between rooms:** the next room's lamp no longer reaches through a wall:
  - on floors, the smooth read of the light map leaves out the tiles beyond a wall of the pixel's own tile (the story's
    wall mask), so two rooms side by side no longer share a band along their wall; arches and doorways keep the smooth
    read across them;
  - on objects, the game's directional light maps now test a room's own lamps against the story's walls, so with a wall
    opened between two rooms the objects behind the part of the wall that still stands stay dark, as the floor in front
    of them does;
  - the window parts' and furniture's reads of the light maps keep to their own side of a wall (a counter top's back
    edge, a door frame).
- **Street light inside the house:** the window pass no longer gives a counter top or another face inside the house plan
  outdoor light near an outside wall. Intended visual change: the outdoor share now stops where the plan's read crosses
  half (the outside wall's line) instead of fading over the last half metre inside.

## Known limitations

- Checked in game on one house (a bedroom, a kitchen and a hall, with the wall between them opened and closed); offline
  on synthetic maps and the captured shaders.
- Where the wall mask cannot tell the sides apart (diagonal walls, rooms lit at a lower detail, a story with no mask
  known) the reads stay as in 2.17.0; a bright room's floor along a dark neighbour's wall can be slightly darker there,
  never lighter.
- Objects drawn by the indoor smooth path (with *Windows take outdoor light* off) still read the directional maps with a
  wide filter across walls; their maps themselves no longer hold the lamp behind the wall.

## Upgrade notes

None: no setting was added or changed. Rooms take the new light when they are lit again (on loading a lot, or after a
lamp or a wall changes).
