# Apex Radiance 2.16.1

[v2.16.1](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.16.1) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

A small hotfix for lamp light that could stay on the ground after lamp changes.

## Fixes

- **Night Lights:** a room-0 solve is kept for Instant lamp lighting only when every light in it is accounted for, so
  old lamp light cannot stay stuck on the ground after lamps are recoloured, moved or switched off.

## Known limitations

- The cause of the stale light seen in 2.16.0 is not confirmed and the new check has not been seen to act in game. If old
  light stays on the ground, *Restart lighting* clears it.
