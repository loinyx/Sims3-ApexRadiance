# Apex Radiance 4.0.12

2026-11-20 · [v4.0.12](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v4.0.12) · ApexRadianceSetup.exe, Apex-Radiance-4.0.12-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**More of a ReShade look runs.** A player's 27-shader look under the RTX Remix bridge lost MXAO and Chromatic
Aberration to Looks' 384 MB cap, and SlowSharp and MicrocontrastReducer did not compile on Direct3D 9.

## Shaders

- **No total memory cap under the RTX Remix bridge.** The 384 MB cap protects the game's 32-bit process; under the
  bridge the effects' render targets and textures live in the bridge's 64-bit process. Without the bridge the cap stays.
- **Loop shaders.** When shader model 3.0 cannot unroll a loop (`X3511`) or run gradients in one, every texture is read
  at level 0 as a last resort, only when every texture of the effect has one level and the same minify and magnify
  filter (then level 0 is the same texel). The original SlowSharp and Hybred's MicrocontrastReducer run.

## Fixes

- **An effect left out for memory stayed out.** It kept its error until the look was loaded again, even after other
  effects were switched off; it is asked again every 300 frames.

## Testing

`tools/looks_fx_check --run`: the two loop shaders (and the gposingway copy of SlowSharp) compile and run on a Direct3D 9
device; 199 pack files give the same result as 4.0.11. In-game runner, a look of SSDO, MXAO, Flair, MicrocontrastReducer
and SlowSharp at 3840x2160: 4.0.11 under the bridge left MXAO and Flair out and the loop shaders could not run; 4.0.12
drew all five ([validation](../validation/looks.md)).

## Known limitations

- The level-0 reads were not compared pixel for pixel with an unrolled build of the same shader (the transformation is
  the one GShade's SlowSharp has by hand).
