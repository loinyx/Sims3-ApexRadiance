# Apex Radiance 1.4.3

Published 2026-09-29 · tag [`v1.4.3`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v1.4.3) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Reset all settings, and a friendlier search.

## New features

- **Reset all settings** (Settings > Menu): puts every feature and the Color settings back to their defaults in one click, after a quick confirmation. Undo brings your previous settings back. The window mode and the menu's own preferences (language, menu key, text size) stay as they are.

## Changes

- **Search ignores accents and letter case:** "ç", "Ç" and "c" find each other, "é" finds "e", and so on, in every menu language.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
