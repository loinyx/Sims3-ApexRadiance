# Apex Radiance 1.0.0

Published 2026-09-28 · tag [`v1.0.0`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

The first release of **Apex Radiance**, the night lighting and visuals mod for The Sims 3, now as its own mod.

Apex used to be a fork of Sims3SettingsSetter and replaced it. Apex Radiance is a separate `ApexRadiance.asi` with its own framework, written from scratch. It runs on its own or next to the **official** Sims3SettingsSetter.

**Credits:** Made by @loinyx. Sims3SettingsSetter by sims3fiend is the mod whose design this framework is modeled on. Third-party code: Dear ImGui, Microsoft Detours, toml++, SMAA, Lucide icons. Full credits in the menu (Settings > About) and the README.

## New features

- **Night Lights:** street and lot lamps light the ground, lots, streets, outdoor objects, fences, walls and roofs, with no hard edge at lot borders. Lamps on upper floors light the yard too.
- **Water & Snow:** lamps glow on ponds, ponds mirror their shore, and walked-on sidewalks show through the snow.
- **Color:** brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, sharpness, smooth gradients, film tones, a color mixer and a vignette.
- **Depth Blur:** softly blurs the distant background and stays sharp in map view.
- **Anti-aliasing:** SMAA or FXAA on the world; menus stay sharp.
- **Borderless window:** windowed or fullscreen.
- **A new menu (Ctrl+Shift+F11):** grouped pages with tabs, a short description on every option, search (Ctrl+F), profiles, undo, per-setting reset, hold Alt to peek at the game, and a hold-to-compare view for Color.

## Known limitations

Requires The Sims 3 Steam 1.67.2 (`TS3W.exe`) for the lighting features.

## Upgrade notes

1. Close the game and the launcher.
2. Put `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\` (an ASI loader is needed).
3. **Coming from Apex Edition / Night Remake:** delete the old `S3SSApex.asi` (or the old combined `Sims3SettingsSetter.asi` build) and install the [official Sims3SettingsSetter](https://github.com/sims3fiend/Sims3SettingsSetter/releases) if you want its features. Your settings are copied over automatically on the first start.
