# Apex Radiance 2.5.2

Published 2026-10-01 · tag [`v2.5.2`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.5.2) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Pond reflections in any weather.

## Fixes

- **Reflections:** the lamp glow and the shore reflection on ponds no longer disappear when the weather is cloudy or rainy (the game draws ponds differently when it is not sunny, and Apex Radiance now recognises both).

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
