# Apex Radiance 2.10.0

Published 2026-10-07 · tag [`v2.10.0`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.10.0) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Lamp posts light upper floors correctly, light behind balconies.

## New features

- **Light behind balconies:** new option (Lighting > Buildings > Walls, on by default): walls behind a balcony get a soft share of the light from the lamps below instead of staying black.
- **Captures take far less space:** old captures are cleaned up automatically (2 GB / 30 folders by default), and big texture dumps are only saved in developer mode.

## Changes

- **Upper floors:** lamps on the ground now light the walls of upper stories the way they should: lower walls only block light below their real top, so stacked and stepped walls no longer go dark with bright streaks at each floor line, and the light fades naturally with distance and when it climbs steeply.
- **Doors and windows:** doors and windows on a foundation now take the light of lamps standing on the ground.
- **Walled rooms:** ground lamp light no longer spills onto the grass inside walled rooms, roofless ones and diagonal walls included.
- **Towers and parapets:** light passing over a balcony parapet now leaves a soft, realistic shadow on the wall above.
- **Faster wall lighting:** Apex's wall tests do less repeated work while lamps are edited.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
