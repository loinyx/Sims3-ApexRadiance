# Apex Radiance 2.15.6

[v2.15.6](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.15.6) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

Continuous winter terrain light and less repeated lighting work.

## Changes

- **Night Lights:** lighting calculations reuse unchanged data without reducing quality. Room maps, fence gaps and gate tests avoid repeated work; room-map drawing batches state changes while preserving the original constants.
- **Performance:** cache compression avoids unnecessary match searches without changing the resulting data.

## Fixes

- **Winter terrain:** frozen and melting terrain keeps continuous light at lot edges in the supported shader layouts, including compact terrain passes used with weather variants.
- **Trees:** lamp brightness and saturation follow the transition through dusk and dawn.
- **Edit Town:** missing outdoor lighting work is restored even while other rooms still have pending updates. Native lighting states and completion remain under the game's control.

## Known limitations

- Texture allocation stalls remain under investigation. The frame report separates device allocation time from the surrounding DDS creation work.
- No controlled gameplay FPS gain is established. Visual coverage across all climates and game versions remains incomplete; unrecognised terrain layouts retain the existing fallback.
- The loading recovery addresses a missing outdoor queue entry, not every possible loading hang.
