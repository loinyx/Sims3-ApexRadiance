# Apex Radiance 2.5.3

Published 2026-10-02 · tag [`v2.5.3`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.5.3) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Faster lighting updates and a clearer Performance menu.

## Changes

- **Room lighting:** Improved coordinated lighting updates across floors and recovery after lot loading. Rooms at Night corrections are retained as rooms finish updating.
- **Smoother camera movement:** Reduced repeated lighting work and spread terrain lighting updates over multiple frames, while keeping the initial world-load rebuild.
- **Performance menu:** Grouped the controls into Camera and lighting, Files and objects, Textures and Sims, and Memory handling, using the mod’s existing style and icons. Main controls remain visible.
- **Cleaner labels:** Removed Experimental badges from the requested performance controls, Rooms at Night adjustment and indoor lighting between floors. Your settings and defaults are unchanged.

## Fixes

- **Paintings above furniture:** Added a correction for paintings becoming dark when furniture is placed underneath them. Further gameplay checks are still needed near walls and stair openings.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
