# Apex Radiance 2.18.4

2026-10-31 · [v2.18.4](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.18.4) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

**Walls' shade on the ground built on the GPU, lamp drags without lag.** The shade that walls, fences and plant crowns
cast on the ground was built on a CPU worker, 2 to 44 ms per lot plus a queue, so a dragged lamp's shadows trailed it
by 0.3 to 0.8 s and the ground flashed when it was let go. It is now built on the GPU in the frame the lamp moves, with
the CPU kept as the fallback ([lot-light-pass](../features/night-lighting/lot-light-pass.md#lamp-shade-on-lot-ground-groundshadow)).

## Changes

- **Wall shade on the ground (GPU).** The lot's shade and the world grass around it are built in pixel shaders: walls
  with doors, windows and lower cells, garden walls, fences, gates, soft wall ends and crowns. Measured per lot: 0.05 ms
  against 2 to 6 ms on a 40 m lot, 0.25 ms against 31 to 44 ms on a lot with 190 walls. A terrain chunk's shade is made
  again over the changed box only, with all mips: a whole chunk takes 0.2 ms against 13 ms. At rest the bytes match the
  CPU within 1/255 on 3 texels across 13 test scenes, chunks bit for bit. Lots past the shader's bounds, or a device
  without the formats, keep the CPU worker.
- **Lamp drags.** Every lot a dragged lamp reaches is shaded again each frame with the lamp at its live place, also for
  posts whose rooms the game still solves. The moving state ends when the drag hold ends, so the ground no longer flashes
  at the let go. A fast drag no longer re-renders its terrain chunk to the same map (4 to 45 ms render-thread spikes).
  A lamp picked up again just after placing it keeps its ground light (a 0.5 s gap before)
  ([terrain-relight](../features/night-lighting/terrain-relight.md#dragged-lamps-on-the-gpu)).
- **Plant crowns.** One plant was recorded again whenever the camera's drift moved it (7962 crowns for 821 plants),
  each copy taking the light once more; copies within 0.5 m across and 2.5 m in height now merge. A crown that holds a
  lamp's head casts no shade for that lamp ([foliage](../features/night-lighting/foliage.md)).
- **Developer tools.** Tester switch *Ground shade on the GPU*; the light capture lists, for every lamp post of the lot,
  the shade around it and the walls and crowns that cut its light ([light-probe](../features/dev-tools/light-probe.md)).

## Fixes

- A lamp copy held from the catalog no longer leaves its light baked where it passed: the terrain stamps also follow the
  lot's baked lamps that no room-0 list holds.

## Known limitations

- Tested in game on one NVIDIA GPU through DXVK; AMD and Intel GPUs were not checked.
- In heavy rain the terrain variants that use all 16 samplers show no wall shade on the world grass, as before.
