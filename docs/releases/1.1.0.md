# Apex Radiance 1.1.0

Published 2026-09-28 · tag [`v1.1.0`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v1.1.0) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Depth Blur, rebuilt.

## New features

- **Auto focus:** whatever the camera looks at stays sharp, and the background blurs more the farther it is. It follows the camera as you zoom and rotate, so zooming in gives a soft portrait-style background and zooming out keeps the whole lot sharp.
- **New controls:** Focus (Auto / Fixed), Blur amount and Sharp area (Small / Medium / Large). Fixed keeps the previous behaviour. The menu now shows Depth Blur's measured GPU cost.

## Changes

- **Smooth, natural transition:** the blur grows gradually with distance instead of fading a blurred copy over the sharp picture.
- **Same look at every resolution:** the blur size scales with your screen height, so it matches at 1080p, 1440p and 4K.
- **Lamps stay bright:** blur is computed in linear light at higher precision, so night lamps glow in the blur and skies have no banding.

## Fixes

- **Clean edges:** trees, roofs and people against a blurred background no longer smear or shimmer when the camera moves.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept; Depth Blur starts in Auto focus.
