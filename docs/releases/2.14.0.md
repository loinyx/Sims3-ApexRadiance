# Apex Radiance 2.14.0

Pending · [v2.14.0](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.14.0) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

Lamp light that climbs stairwells, stairs that match their floor, arches without seams, trees lit leaf by leaf,
moonlight that no longer builds up, and memory and save fixes.

## New features

- **Light through stairwells:** a lamp below a stairwell lights the walls above it with a soft edge, and the landing
  and the rooms above take the light reflected up through the opening. A room lit only from another floor looks
  brighter, the way eyes adjust to the dark, but never as bright as a room with its own lamp. A lamp in a closed room
  next to the stairwell no longer shines through its walls onto the floor above. Part of *Indoor light between floors*.
- **Stairs on the room's light:** stairs and objects against a wall no longer take the colour of a lit room behind the
  wall; a stair with the floor's texture has the floor's colour, and its top steps take the light of the floor they lead
  to. Part of *Smooth indoor light*.
- **Light through arches and doorways:** the floor running through an arch has no colour step, also after changing
  floors, and a lamp's light through an arch or door has a soft edge while keeping the opening's shape. Part of
  *Indoor light through openings* and *Smooth light maps*.
- **Trees and bushes lit leaf by leaf:** each leaf takes the light of its own distance, height and side towards each
  lamp, light passes through the leaves, and a lamp at the foot of a tree lights the trunk and the lower crown. A small
  lamp near a large tree lights only the branches within its reach, no longer the whole crown in its colour. Each tree
  takes the lamps around it by the same rule as walls and objects (reach, walls in the way, lamps shining out of a
  window), in each lamp's own colour, with no white glare next to a bright lamp. Tree and bush crowns softly shade lamp light on the lot's ground. Part of *Realistic lamp reach* and *Walls shade lamp light
  near the ground*.

## Changes

- **Indoor light no longer changes as the camera moves.** Light through openings and between floors no longer depends
  on which stairs and railings are on screen, so rooms near openings are not lit again every second or two.
- **Lamps that turn on by themselves** (at dusk or dawn) update their own room first.
- **Smoother room lighting after a load:** the extra room updates no longer start a step that does not fit in the
  frame's budget.
- **Lighting updates repeat less work** and do not wait for the graphics card inside the drawing of a frame.
- **Game memory:** the game trims its unused memory every minute and before saving, and takes its large blocks from the
  top of the address space (Steam 1.67.2).
- **Room to save:** releases idle buffers earlier during saving; completed compression storage and deferred frees are
  released while pending compression writes stay protected. Early save preparation requires the verified Steam
  executable; other builds keep the existing world-save preparation.
- **Compatibility with sims3-modernization:** Room to save also prepares saves when its `ts3native.asi` is installed.
- **Screen effects:** avoid unused image copies and repeated timing work. The fallback scene copy is created on demand,
  and effect timing queries are sampled rather than issued every frame. Camera values are reused while the projection is
  identified. Effect quality is unchanged.
- **Object and compression caches:** reduce repeated work with bounded memory. Repeated texture strips reuse identical
  encoded bytes, ordered CAS lists skip sorting, and object and type lookups keep validated cached results. Idle
  compression storage is retired and active output copies have a shared budget.

## Fixes

- **Moonlight no longer builds up:** the night could come out about 35% brighter, and differ between loads, when a
  game transition started from the already scaled value. The moonlight factor is now applied where the game reads the
  value, once.
- **A lamp switched off over a stairwell** no longer leaves its light on the floor below.
- **The floor beside a stairwell opening** no longer shows a lit strip.
- **Instant lamp lighting:** a new or moved outdoor lamp that the game has not yet listed on a floor now lights that
  floor as well.
- **Night Lights:** wall support rows cannot borrow lamp light through a closed floor, and light imported through an
  opening uses the receiving room's normalisation.
- **Object lookup cache:** expired local entries can obtain the refreshed shared entry instead of repeating native
  searches indefinitely.

## Known limitations

- The new behaviours have no menu option of their own. They can be turned off only through the setting they belong to,
  which also turns off everything else under it.
- Upper floors lit only through a stairwell stay dim, and dark floors there stay dark: they receive reflected light
  only. Light reflected up a stairwell may count some of the same light twice.
- Some rooms, seen on a bathroom, can be drawn with the game's own blue unlit colour; the cause is not yet known.
- Error 12 is not eliminated. The game remains constrained by its 32-bit address space, and save memory endpoints do
  not measure the peak. Native cache accounting is distinct from RAM returned to Windows.
- No substantial FPS or RAM reduction is established by the offline checks; the release build requires its own in-game
  validation.
- Early save dispatch, the allocator changes and partial native cache control are validated only for Steam 1.67. Other
  supported builds keep signature-resolved existing paths; this does not establish identical internal layouts across
  versions.
