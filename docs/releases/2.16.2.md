# Apex Radiance 2.16.2

[v2.16.2](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.16.2) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

Smoother panning as the neighbourhood fills in. Houses come into full detail with less stutter, lots at the edge of the
view keep their detail, and evenings with long garden fences cost less frame time.

## Changes

- **Lot Streaming:** lots that load together share one build time per frame (6 ms, 4 ms while the camera moves), so a
  group of lots coming into detail no longer stalls a frame. It comes with *Spread lot lighting while moving*; the lot
  being played keeps the game's own build time.
- **Night Lights:** fence shadows on the lawn and the half-float vertex and texture reads use exact replacements for two
  slow library calls; the light is the same, bit for bit, at a fraction of the cost.

## Fixes

- **Keep lot visibility stable** keeps lots at the edge of the detail distance in full detail (about 11% farther than
  where a lot is promoted); before, it removed the game's small margin instead.
- **Smooth lot streaming** keeps the game's transition throttle working with up to 16 detailed lots; above 12 it had
  turned itself off.

## Known limitations

- The shared build time cannot split a single load stage; the first stage of a large lot can still take a long frame.
- Most of a lot's time to full detail is spent waiting for its lighting, which this release does not change.
