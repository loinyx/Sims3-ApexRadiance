# Apex Radiance 1.4.8

Published 2026-09-29 · tag [`v1.4.8`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v1.4.8) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Color works with shader-replacing mods.

## Changes

- **Better logs for bug reports:** `ApexRadiance_LOG.txt` now also records:
  - your graphics card and driver;
  - the mods and wrappers in `Game\Bin` (with sizes, dates and versions);
  - the game's graphics options (`Options.ini`);
  - Color's settings when they are saved, and a short Color status line every minute.

  When you report a problem, please attach `ApexRadiance_LOG.txt` from `Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 3\Apex Radiance\`.

## Fixes

- **Found the cause of "Color looks blown out and the sliders do nothing":** mods that replace the game's shaders (such as **D3DShaderReplacer**) could also swap Apex's own Color shader while the game loaded. Apex now checks that its shaders really are its own after creating them; if another mod swapped one, it logs it and creates a marked copy that the replacer won't recognise, so both mods can be used together. Thanks Elix for all the testing!
- Color now sets its shader before its settings, and double-checks after each change that its settings reached the graphics card.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
