# Apex Radiance 2.2.0

Published 2026-09-30 · tag [`v2.2.0`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.2.0) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Banding Fix, and a lighter Ambient Occlusion with more qualities and map view.

## New features

- **Banding Fix (new, on by default):** no more color steps (banding) in lamp light, shadows and other smooth gradients. The game rounds its picture to 256 shades per color, which draws rings around lamp light on walls and floors; Apex now adds an invisible, fixed grain right where the colors are rounded, so light fades smoothly on every surface of the 3D world. Menus are untouched.
- **Ambient Occlusion in map view:** soft shade around houses and trees when the map is open ("Also in map view", on by default).
- **Show the shade alone:** a new Advanced switch shows only the Ambient Occlusion while you adjust it.

## Changes

- **New Color › Banding tab:** the Banding Fix's Strength and Smooth gradients together. Smooth gradients moved here from Picture and now works with Picture off too; it also softens the steps in the sky.
- **Ambient Occlusion is lighter:** about 25-30% less work on the graphics card for the same look (4K, in testing), and it is no longer marked Experimental; a note says it is heavier than the other effects.
- **Five Ambient Occlusion qualities:** Very Low, Low, Medium, High and Ultra, now right on the card.

## Upgrade notes

**Ambient Occlusion settings reset once** to the new defaults after this update.

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
