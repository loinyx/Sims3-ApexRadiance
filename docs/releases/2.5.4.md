# Apex Radiance 2.5.4

Published 2026-10-02 · tag [`v2.5.4`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.5.4) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Hotfix: responsive lighting and continuous lot edges.

## New features

- **Restore defaults:** Added access to default settings on every page and a whole-mod reset in Settings, with confirmation and Undo. Saved captures, reports and profiles are preserved.

## Changes

- **Faster light changes:** Improved the response when switching all lights on or off, changing their colors or adjusting intensity on a visible lot. Small intensity adjustments and repeated changes are no longer missed by the lighting correction.
- **Smoother lighting updates:** Prioritized the affected terrain areas and grouped rapid edits to reduce unnecessary lighting work, while retaining the coordinated floor lighting and Rooms at Night corrections.
- **Capture feedback:** Object capture ends after one click and returns to the main panel. Recording, startup and capture notices share a centered position. Updated controls and instructions are available in English, Portuguese, Spanish and French.

## Fixes

- **Lighting after lot loading:** Newly visible lots refresh their outdoor lighting locally. Switching off a lamp also clears its previous light contribution instead of leaving the ground illuminated until another switch.
- **Continuous light at lot edges:** Fixed an outdoor terrain rendering path that could show different lighting across the lot boundary. Light pools remain consistent as they extend beyond the lot.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
