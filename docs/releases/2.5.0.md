# Apex Radiance 2.5.0

Published 2026-10-01 · tag [`v2.5.0`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.5.0) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Report a problem: captures, sessions and screenshots.

## New features

- **Report a problem (new page, System):** everything that helps fix a bug, explained step by step. Save a report (the log and your settings), record a few seconds of the lighting, capture the light at a spot under the mouse, or save a lighting snapshot; each goes into its own dated folder in Documents › Electronic Arts › The Sims 3 › Apex Radiance › Captures, and nothing is ever overwritten.
- **Capture sessions:** start a session, save as many captures as you need, then end it: one folder with all of them, the log and your settings, ready to zip and send.
- **Screenshots:** every capture also saves a picture of the screen, without the Apex menu (on by default; "Include a screenshot").
- **Your captures:** the page lists every saved capture with its date, time and size, with buttons to open its folder or delete it.
- **On-screen notes:** a note in the top-left corner shows when a recording starts, its seconds, and when a capture is saved.

## Changes

- **Keys:** the recording, light capture and lighting snapshot keys follow your shortcut set (F6, F7 and F8 with the F keys) and are listed in Settings › Shortcuts.
- **Start note:** the note with the menu key at every start can now be turned off in Settings › Menu.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
