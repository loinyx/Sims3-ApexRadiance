# Apex Radiance 2.9.0

Published 2026-10-07 · tag [`v2.9.0`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.9.0) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

21 languages, steadier game, better lamp light.

## New features

- **Languages:** the menu now speaks every language The Sims 3 supports (21, including Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Russian and more) and picks your Windows language automatically; you can still change it in Settings. Long texts shorten neatly and show in full on hover.

## Changes

- **Lamp cones:** lamp light cones are now shaped per pixel, and cone light spills softly onto the ground.
- **Foundations:** foundation faces are lit at their real height.
- **Profiles:** every option on the Lighting page is now on in all starting profiles. Street lamps lit in the lot light, lot grass keeping its lamp light and relighting lots at dusk are now on by default and no longer Experimental, as are light through doors and windows and smooth indoor light.

## Fixes

- **Fewer “Runtime Error!” closes:** Apex now catches its own errors instead of ending the game, and the crash report says more about what happened.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
