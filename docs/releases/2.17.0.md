# Apex Radiance 2.17.0

[v2.17.0](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.17.0) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

Lamp edits stay on their own lot, and Room to save asks once whether to stay on.

## New features

- **Room to save:** after an update or a first install, a card at the top of the screen asks once whether to keep it
  on and explains it: it keeps 128 MB of the game's memory free and hands it back right before each save, so saves
  stop failing with Error 12 in long sessions. The card takes mouse input only and leaves the menu closed.

## Changes

- **Faster room lighting:** a lamp edit sends again the rooms near stairs and openings of its own lot only. Before,
  every loaded lot sent them again after each edit (in a measured minute, 147 lamp edits sent 1053 rooms); rooms on
  the edited lot still update, including the story below a stair opening.

## Known limitations

- The game's own memory is measured but not reduced: its allocator, script heap and driver take most of the 32-bit
  address space, and freeing more would need changes inside the game's allocator.
- The frame-time gain of the lamp-edit change was not measured with a representative recording; the published figure
  is the number of rooms sent.
