# Apex Radiance 2.18.2

2026-10-29 · [v2.18.2](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.18.2) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

**Lamp posts light neighbouring lots, and a stair fix.** Lamp posts now light the walls of the lots around them, not
only their own lot. With Instant (GPU), the 4 nearest lots are relit on the GPU like the active lot: every story at
once, during the drag.

## New features

- **Lamp posts on neighbouring lots.** Lamp posts light the outside walls, floors and ground of neighbouring lots, on
  every story ([level-light-share](../features/night-lighting/level-light-share.md#lamp-posts-of-other-lots)).

## Changes

- **Instant (GPU) on neighbouring lots.** The 4 nearest lots follow a dragged post in the same frame, 8 to 31 ms per
  move, with no room solves; upper stories update together with the ground floor, never one story at a time; placing or
  dropping a post shows on them with no delay; they cost 0.006 to 0.014 ms a frame
  ([rt-outdoor-lamps](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md)).
- With Classic, or on lots farther away, the post reaches them about 1.3 s after it stops.

## Fixes

- Upper steps of an outdoor stair beside a wall no longer take light from lamps behind the wall
  ([fences](../features/night-lighting/fences.md)).

## Known limitations

- Only lamp posts reach another lot; sconces, spots and shaded lamps light their own lot.
- The walls of neighbouring lots cast no shadow of their own on the posts' light (the shadows are the active lot's).
- A lamp post standing just past its lot's edge can leave the grass around it dark
  ([lot-light-pass](../features/night-lighting/lot-light-pass.md#limitations)).
