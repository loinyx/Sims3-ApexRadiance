# Apex Radiance 4.1.0

Unpublished · release/4.1.0 · ApexLauncher.exe (also attached as ApexRadianceSetup.exe for older Setups' update), Apex-Radiance-4.1.0-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**Apex Radiance Setup becomes Apex Launcher.** The installer is now the place the game is started from: Play from
Home, see the game's health at a glance, switch mods on and off, pick a world or a save, and look through every picture
and video in a Gallery with albums and a scrapbook. It runs without administrator rights and asks only when a change
needs them. `ApexRadiance.asi` is unchanged from 4.0.x.

## Changes

- **Home and Play** ([Apex Launcher](../features/installer.md#the-launcher-200)): *Continue where you left off* shows
  the save to go back to and starts the game straight into it; *Another save* picks a different one. Updates go in
  first (or *Play without updating*). One line shows the game's health and how the last session ended, with *Start
  again into this save* after a crash or low memory; while playing, the playing time and the memory used. The other
  households each play into their own save, then a row of pictures. The launcher waits in the notification area while
  the game runs.
- **Components:** Apex Radiance on its own card, then its parts (Sims 3 Modernization, Graphics, frame generation and
  the frame limit, TS3Wrap) and the mods by other modders as rows with their state, and menus for the channel, the
  renderer and the frame limit. A dot marks each row Apply changes. TS3Wrap (the restart host) is no longer
  experimental: it is on in a new install unless the player turns it off.
- **Health:** a crystal in the game's state colour, the checks by area, automatic fixes, restore points and the last
  session. Blocked only when Apex Radiance itself cannot load; suggestions have their own button.
- **Mods:** every mod with its kind, an on/off switch, a list and a grid, tags, notes, merge and split, a search over
  folders and tags, and a finder for the mod behind a problem; default replacements are the mods that replace an
  object, a Create a Sim part or a texture; mod fixes contributed by @alexvixell35-stack.
- **Worlds and Saves:** the worlds to start a game in, and each save with its household photo, Play this save and a
  backup.
- **Gallery:** pictures and videos from wherever Apex saves them, by day or household, with favourites, captions,
  search, albums and a scrapbook board; Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo.
- **Settings:** *Install updates when I press Play*; the picture on top of Home (a gallery picture, the household's
  photo or none), its height and strength; the window background (Mica, a stronger Mica, Acrylic or a solid colour).
- **Look:** light or dark and the accent colour follow Windows.
- **Install and uninstall:** Start menu and desktop shortcuts and an *Installed apps* entry that removes everything the
  launcher installed. A removal that fails half way keeps the install record true, so a retry is safe.
- **Smooth Motion:** its card warns that recording with the NVIDIA app freezes the game, and to record with OBS or
  Apex's recorder.

## Known limitations

- Not yet verified on a real install: the upgrade from Setup 1.2.x, the administrator prompt on a game under Program
  Files, a full uninstall, Play with the EA app edition, *Continue* opening the save in game, the Acrylic and stronger
  Mica backgrounds.
- The texts new in 4.1.0 are translated into Portuguese; the other languages show them in English until they are
  translated.
- The game's own `Video.avi` recordings show without a picture when Windows has no decoder for them.

## Upgrade notes

- Apex Radiance Setup 1.2.x offers the update itself; its settings, games and language move to
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Apex Launcher`, and the launcher asks once whether to install itself or stay portable.
- The launcher ships as 2.0.23, so the earlier test builds update to it.
- No setting of Apex Radiance moves.
