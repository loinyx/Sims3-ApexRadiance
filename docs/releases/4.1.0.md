# Apex Radiance 4.1.0

Unpublished · release/4.1.0 · ApexLauncher.exe (also attached as ApexRadianceSetup.exe for older Setups' update), Apex-Radiance-4.1.0-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**Apex Radiance Setup becomes Apex Launcher.** The installer is now the place the game is started from: Play from
Home, see the game's health at a glance, switch mods on and off, pick a world or a save, and look through every picture
and video in a Gallery with albums and a scrapbook. It runs without administrator rights and asks only when a change
needs them. `ApexRadiance.asi` is unchanged from 4.0.x.

## Changes

- **Home and Play** ([Apex Launcher](../features/installer.md#the-launcher-200)): the newest save's picture with Play
  on it (updates go in first, or *Play without updating*), health, the last session and counts; each household plays
  straight into its save; the launcher waits in the notification area while the game runs.
- **Components:** Apex Radiance with its parts around it (Sims 3 Modernization, the renderer, frame generation, the
  restart host), and the mods by other modders as a list. The restart host is no longer experimental: it is on in a
  new install unless the player turns it off.
- **Health:** a crystal in the game's state colour, the checks by area, automatic fixes, restore points and the last
  session.
- **Mods:** every mod with its kind, an on/off switch, a list and a grid, tags, notes, merge and split, and a finder for
  the mod behind a problem; mod fixes contributed by @alexvixell35-stack.
- **Worlds and Saves:** the worlds to start a game in, and each save with its household photo, Play this save and a
  backup.
- **Gallery:** pictures and videos from wherever Apex saves them, by day or household, with favourites, captions,
  search, albums and a scrapbook board; Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo.
- **Look:** light or dark and the accent colour follow Windows.
- **Install and uninstall:** Start menu and desktop shortcuts and an *Installed apps* entry that removes everything the
  launcher installed.
- **Smooth Motion:** its card warns that recording with the NVIDIA app freezes the game, and to record with OBS or
  Apex's recorder.

## Known limitations

- Not yet verified on a real install: the upgrade from Setup 1.2.x, the administrator prompt on a game under Program
  Files, a full uninstall, Play with the EA app edition.
- The texts new in 4.1.0 are translated into Portuguese; the other languages show them in English until they are
  translated.
- The game's own `Video.avi` recordings show without a picture when Windows has no decoder for them.

## Upgrade notes

- Apex Radiance Setup 1.2.x offers the update itself; its settings, games and language move to
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Apex Launcher`, and the launcher asks once whether to install itself or stay portable.
- The launcher ships as 2.0.1, so the 2.0.0 test builds update to it.
- No setting of Apex Radiance moves.
