# Apex Radiance 1.3.0

Published 2026-09-29 · tag [`v1.3.0`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v1.3.0) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Smoother gameplay: fewer micro-stutters.

This release goes after the small, frequent stutters of The Sims 3, especially while moving the camera and zooming out. Every change was measured in game first; the game still looks exactly the same.

## New features

- **New Performance page** (System > Performance) with switches for each optimization.
- **Experimental (off by default):** faster texture compression (bit-identical results to the game's own) and faster cache compression. Both check themselves against the game at the start of each session and turn off if anything differs.

## Changes

- **Faster resource lookups:** the game used to search every package (around 300 with mods) one by one each time it needed a texture or model. Apex Radiance now remembers where things are, so lookups are about twice as cheap and stutters caused by them dropped by roughly 70%.
- **Lot lighting spread out while the camera moves:** instead of spending up to 15 ms of one frame on lot lighting, the work is split across frames while you move the camera. Lighting stutters while panning dropped by about 80%. When the camera stops, the game's normal budget comes back.
- **Apex's shaders are compiled at startup** on a background thread, never in the middle of play.

## Fixes

- **Fewer big freezes from Night Lights:** lamps that switch or flicker by themselves no longer trigger full terrain light rebuilds, rebuilds never happen while the camera moves, and they're never repeated right after the game rebuilt on its own.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
