# Apex Radiance 2.15.2

2026-10-22 · [v2.15.2](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.15.2) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

Moving the camera at night is smooth again with Instant lamp lighting.

## Fixes

- **Frame rate with Instant lamp lighting:** 2.15.1 gave the record of each baked lamp a flag that left padding bytes in
  it. Those records are compared and hashed byte by byte, so every lamp looked changed every frame, and the share of
  light coming from under the floors was worked out again for every floor texel in each frame: frames of 30 to 90 ms
  while the camera moved at night. The flag is now 32 bits and the build checks that the record has no padding
  ([outdoor lamps on the GPU](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md)).

## Known limitations

- The game's own room solving can still take up to its full budget in some frames right after a lot loads or when a
  lamp switches anywhere in town; Apex's lighting work inside those solves was measured but not changed in this version.
