# Apex Radiance 2.0.0

Published 2026-09-30 · tag [`v2.0.0`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.0.0) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Indoor light between floors, Rooms at Night, faster room lighting, custom shortcuts and Extreme anti-aliasing.

**A big, experimental update:** much of how houses are lit at night is new. If a room ever looks wrong, use "Refresh the lighting" (Lighting > Buildings > Rooms at Night, or its shortcut), and please report it.

## New features

- **Indoor light between floors (Experimental):** lamps inside now shine through stairwells and open floors to the floor above or below, and stop at solid floors and walls. Walls, floors, furniture and stairs all take it. Lighting > Stories.
- **Rooms at Night (Experimental):** rooms with every lamp off no longer glow strong blue. Set the soft background light yourself with Brightness and Blue tint; it applies at once to walls, floors and furniture, and lamps still add their light on top of it. Lighting > Buildings.
- **Faster Room Lighting (Experimental):** rooms light up sooner when you enter a lot or change floors. Performance page.
- **Keyboard shortcuts your way:** pick a shortcut set (letters, numbers or F keys) or your own keys in the new Shortcuts tab; the first start asks which menu key you want.
- **Edge Smoothing: new Extreme quality** for SMAA and FXAA: straighter long edges (made for 4K) and also edges between colors of the same brightness. At 1080p the card also shows how to get the smoothest image with your graphics driver.

## Changes

- **Changing floors keeps the light:** every floor of the lot you play is lit in full detail, walls above and below the floor line meet with no step in the light, and rooms keep their light when nothing changed, so the short flicker when you switch floors is gone.
- **Furniture takes the room's light:** sofas, beds, tables and other furniture indoors are lit smoothly by the room's lamps and follow the same Brightness and Blue tint as the walls. Furniture in a closed room no longer takes light from a lamp on another floor.
- **The lighting updates by itself:** a few seconds after loading, a moment after you change any lighting setting, and when a lamp is switched on, off or moved. Rooms also stop showing the light of lamps you switched off.
- **New look:** the new logo in the menu, and a note with your menu key at every start.
- **Picture:** the picture filters no longer cost anything while turned off.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
