# Apex Radiance 1.4.1

Published 2026-09-29 · tag [`v1.4.1`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v1.4.1) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Menu languages and better profiles.

## New features

- **The menu now speaks Portuguese, Spanish and French** besides English. Pick it in **Settings > Menu > Language**; "Automatic" follows your Windows language. It switches at once, and the search finds options by their English or translated name.
- **Open the Profiles folder** from the same page, to share your profiles or take them to another PC.

## Changes

- **Profiles: choose what to save and what to load.** When saving, tick the parts to keep: Night Lights, Color, Depth Blur, Edge Smoothing, window mode and, new, Performance. Each saved profile lists what it has, and Load lets you pick which of those parts to apply (Undo still works).

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
