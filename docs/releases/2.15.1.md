# Apex Radiance 2.15.1

Pending · [v2.15.1](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.15.1) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

Placing a lot in Edit Town no longer stays on "Processing", the moon lights the town from higher up so roads no longer
show dark stripes at night, lot area lights reach the ground past the lot border, and lamps hidden behind objects no
longer reflect on water.

## Changes

- **Smoother lot loading:** right after a load or after entering a lot, the extra lighting work that corrects the lot's
  rooms quickly is dosed by the frame time, so it no longer drops the frame rate on PCs without room to spare. The light
  of each room is unchanged; on a slower PC a room may take a little longer to correct. The light from stairwells also
  costs less to compute ([lot lighting while moving](../features/performance/lot-lighting-motion.md)).
- **Moonlight** is 0.277 by default and in the Performance, Balanced and Quality presets (it was 1.0 by default and 1.817
  in the presets): with the moon now higher, the ground takes its light.
- **Instant lamp lighting** relights a map the game writes again and again at most every 0.5 s (a single write is still
  relit at once). A lot that stayed loading made the game write its maps about ten times a second, each relight 45 ms on
  the render thread.

## Fixes

- **Moon:** at night the moon stands at least 20 degrees high. The game keeps its light at the horizon all night (0.7
  degrees after dusk), where every bump of a road and every road piece's slight tilt switched it on or off and curbs cast
  shadows tens of metres long: dark stripes across roads, much darker than the snow beside them. Its shadows are now
  shorter and roads and snow take the same light; by day and at dusk the sun is the game's
  ([night lighting](../features/night-lighting/README.md)).

- **Edit Town:** a placed lot no longer stays on "Processing". A lot finishes loading once the first wall shading pass
  of each of its stories has run; *Wall shading waits while moving* kept starting its 2 s wait over whenever the camera
  read looked still for a moment, and other lots' refinements took the one pass a frame. The wait now keeps its start
  while a first pass is pending, and refinements wait for it ([wall-shading-while-moving.md](../features/performance/wall-shading-while-moving.md)).
- **Lot area lights:** the rectangle and disc area lights of a lot (a park's lights, for instance) light the ground
  around the lot too, at the share of the ground they face, instead of stopping in a straight line at the lot border
  ([terrain-relight.md](../features/night-lighting/terrain-relight.md)).
- **Water:** a lamp behind a tent, a wall or another object no longer shows its reflection on ponds and lakes; its glow
  on the water stays ([water.md](../features/night-lighting/water.md)).

## Known limitations

- Why the camera read flipped between moving and still in Edit Town is not known.
- The lamp reflection on water is hidden by what the scenery reflection's ray meets; without scene depth (Depth Blur and
  Ambient Occlusion off) lamps are never hidden.
