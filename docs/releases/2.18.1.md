# Apex Radiance 2.18.1

[v2.18.1](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.18.1) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

**A hotfix for fences, dragged lamps and rainy dusks.** Removing a whole fence, deleting a lamp right after dragging
another and the end of dusk in the rain now update the ground light at once and without steps.

## Fixes

- A fence type removed whole takes its shadow with it within about half a second (it stayed up to 20 s): with the
  camera still, a fence group that stops being drawn was removed.
- A lamp deleted right after dragging another lamp of the same type on the same lot no longer leaves its light on the
  ground: only the dragged lamps' own changes skip the terrain relight.
- Rainy ground no longer jumps from the game's blocky lamp light to the smoothed one at the end of dusk: the switch
  happens as dusk begins, when the lamps' light on the ground is at its faintest.
