# Apex Radiance 1.4.4

Published 2026-09-29 · tag [`v1.4.4`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v1.4.4) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Better diagnostics for Color.

## New features

- **Color tells you when it can't run:** if Color is on but isn't being applied for a couple of seconds, the Color card now shows a note saying why (for example, another mod taking over the frame, or the game drawing its frames somewhere else). The same line goes to the log.

## Changes

- **The log records what you change in the menu** (and when you Undo it), plus when Color turns on, turns off and starts being applied. If something doesn't seem to work, the log now shows the whole story.

## Upgrade notes

If a setting seems not to stick, check that you didn't click **Undo** in the small note that appears at the bottom right after each change: it puts the previous value back.

When you report a problem, please attach `ApexRadiance_LOG.txt` (and `ApexRadiance_Crash.txt` after a crash) from `Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 3\Apex Radiance\`.

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
