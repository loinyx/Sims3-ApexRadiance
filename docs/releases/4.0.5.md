# Apex Radiance 4.0.5

2026-11-18 · [v4.0.5](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v4.0.5) · ApexRadianceSetup.exe, Apex-Radiance-4.0.5-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**A simpler Shaders page.** Image > Shaders has two tabs instead of three: a look is built and its shaders added on the
Look tab, downloads of packs not on this PC included, and the Installed shaders tab manages what is on the PC. The Apex
menu no longer draws into an off-screen pass of the game, a likely cause of its occasional blink.

## New features

- **New look.** In the Look list: a name, Start empty or Copy this look, then Create; an empty look shows "No shaders
  yet: use Add shaders to build this look" ([Looks](../features/looks.md#menu)).
- **Get more packs.** A card on the Installed shaders tab with ReShade's packs not on this PC, a search, and Download per
  pack with its progress in the row.

## Changes

- **Two tabs, Look and Installed shaders.** The Packs tab and its picking are gone; Library is now Installed shaders.
- **Add shaders in a modal.** Opened from *Shaders in this look*: search, Kind, the shaders by source, the shaders of
  packs not on this PC (adding one downloads its pack, the progress on the pack's group), *Import .fx…* and *Done*. It
  stays open for several adds and fits a 720p screen with 200% text.
- **One Save.** *Shaders in this look* ends with *Add shaders* and *Save*; *Save as a new look…* is in its menu.
- **A missing shader's row** has an alert icon in the grip's column and its action at the row's end, lined up with the
  other rows.
- **Header.** The frame-time pill keeps a fixed width, so a changing frame rate no longer moves the search field.

## Fixes

- **The Apex menu waits for the frame's own EndScene.** It was drawn at the first EndScene of a frame whatever its
  render target; an off-screen pass that ended first (a reflection, a Sim's portrait) took the menu for that frame.

## Known limitations

- The menu's blink is fixed by a likely cause, not a proven one: the in-game run with the menu open logged no
  off-screen EndScene before the frame's own. When frames have no EndScene on the back buffer, the menu waits two
  frames before taking any EndScene, as before.
- The new Shaders page was checked offline (`shaders_menu_test`, 3024 page and 945 modal cases) and in the live viewer,
  not in game.
- The in-game runner's Looks jobs name a built-in look no longer shipped and fail until they name a look of the tester's.

## Upgrade notes

- No setting moves. A look's file and Apex's settings are read as before.
