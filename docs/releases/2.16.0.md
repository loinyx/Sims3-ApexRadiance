# Apex Radiance 2.16.0

[v2.16.0](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.16.0) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

Smoother gradients with a high-precision scene, and outdoor lamps that are brightest where they stand: strong lamps
light the ground and walls beside them at full strength, and their light enters rooms through doors and windows
dimmer than outside, falling off into the room.

## New features

- **Banding Fix: High-precision scene.** The scene is drawn into a 16-bit target and dithered once when it is resolved,
  so dark gradients and lamp glows show fewer steps. On by default.

## Changes

- **Banding Fix:** the card is redesigned. *Smooth gradients* and *Moving grain* are removed; the grain stays inside the
  colour range, uses a finer pattern, leaves the menu and other interface passes alone and is halved on blended passes.
- **Night Lights:** with Instant lamp lighting, the lot ground, outdoor floors and outside walls show the light of a
  strong lamp past the old cap at full white, instead of a flat patch around it.
- **Night Lights:** outdoor lamp light through doors and windows is no brighter than the surface outside the opening,
  falls off from the opening into the room, keeps the lamp's colour and fades out at the back of the room instead of
  ending in a strip.
- **Night Lights:** a lamp mounted on a wall lights the wall it hangs on.

## Fixes

- **Night Lights:** room light no longer shows lines, steps or a dark band beside walls and door frames, and the light of
  a yard no longer leaks along the inside of a wall.
- **Night Lights:** coloured lamps keep their colour where their light is strongest, instead of turning yellow or white.
- **Night Lights:** the light that windows let into rooms no longer changes with the camera angle.
- **Night Lights:** editing an outdoor lamp updates the rooms that see it through doors and windows.
- **Night Lights:** gables keep the wall shade strip as the game maps it.

## Known limitations

- Light past the old cap needs Instant lamp lighting on a device with two simultaneous render targets. Classic, other
  lots, winter and snowy floors, objects and the lower-quality outside wall shaders keep the cap.
- The changes to room light, windows and wall lamps passed offline checks; in-game confirmation is partial. The cost of
  the new room light map read (about 20 texture reads per room-lit pixel) is not measured.
- Where the light past the old cap is not shown, a strong near-white lamp keeps its hue at its hottest spot (slightly
  tinted) instead of clipping to white.
- With the High-precision scene, an overlay that draws through the game's own Direct3D 9 device inside Present (only
  without the game's DXVK) may not show; turn the High-precision scene off if it does.
- Light through a window lands as a soft patch, not a sharp outline of the window (the light map's resolution).
- When a lamp's colour changes, the world grass and roads beside the lot can show blocks of the old colour for a few
  seconds while the terrain light is rebuilt.
