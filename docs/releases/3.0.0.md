# Apex Radiance 3.0.0

2026-11-09 · [v3.0.0](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v3.0.0) · ApexRadiance.asi, Apex-Radiance-RTX-Remix-bridge.zip

## Highlights

**Indoor lights run on the GPU.** On Steam 1.67.2 and EA app 1.69.47, Lamp lighting: Instant (GPU) draws indoor lamps as well as outdoor ones, so a
lamp edit shows on walls, floors, ceilings, furniture and stairs with no wait for the room's solve
([gpu-room-lamps](../features/night-lighting/gpu-room-lamps.md)). In every mode, lamp light passes through diagonal walls and
each opening's real shape, and double-height rooms and attics under a roof light their upper walls in the room's own tone.

**Light between rooms.** On GPU-drawn rooms, furniture shadows run on through arches, floors keep one tone across them, and a lit
room gives the next one a soft light through each arch ([arches](../features/night-lighting/arches.md)).

**A 64-bit renderer, optional.** The release carries the RTX Remix bridge, set to plain DXVK: the renderer runs in its own 64-bit
process and the game keeps about 600 MB more free memory ([rtx-remix-bridge](../features/rtx-remix-bridge.md)).

**A new default look.** The lighting and picture defaults change, and an update moves the lighting ones once, leaving any value a
player chose.

## New features

- **GPU room lamps.** Each room solve of the active lot is kept: the base without lamps, each lamp's tests, the samples and the
  game's 3D records. The GPU draws every map from each lamp's law, on by default with the physical model. Colour, intensity,
  switch, move and new-lamp edits solve no room in the frame of the edit; a settle solves the rooms 2 s later for their
  normalisation and bounce. Against the game's solves, 95.46% of 2,691,056 lamp texels in 10 recordings are within 2/255.
- **Table legs.** 205 capsules for 36 table models, baked from the game's meshes, cast a soft streak. Past 1.5 m over the leg's top
  the streak fades and widens, gone at 3 m, so a ceiling lamp adds a faint soft foot to the furniture's soft shadow.
- **Stairs in the shadow tiles.** Stairs by floor holes cast a shadow, up to 4,096 triangles a lot.
- **Sharper floor light.** Floor maps at twice their size, 4 sub-texels a texel, 16-bit where the device filters it: the 25 cm
  steps of light through doors and the diamonds on decks outside diagonal doors are gone.
- **Light bounces between rooms.** A room takes, per texel, the form factor of each arch times the part of the neighbour's
  ambient its lamps gave, after the tone and with no solve, so it follows a drag. It adds at most 0.9 of a level. Rooms at Night
  > *Light bounces between rooms* (`luzRefletidaEntreComodos`), on by default, in Reset and the presets, applied live.

- **RTX Remix bridge.** A separate download, `Apex-Radiance-RTX-Remix-bridge.zip`: NVIDIA's bridge with patches for The Sims 3
  and DXVK 3.1.1 x64 in the server process. Settings > Compatibility shows it as installed, and while DXVK runs inside the game it
  offers the download under *Optional*; there is no start note.

## Changes

- **Lamp drags.** Rooms the GPU draws are held out of the solve queue during a drag. A drag keeps one quality until 750 ms
  still. Directional maps are kept per lamp, so only moved lamps are traced. Rigs are gathered again every 100 ms of a drag and
  room reflection cubes are taken again in turn, so metal follows the lamp ([lamp-drags](../features/night-lighting/lamp-drags.md)).
- **Furniture shadows.** A table moves with the lamp standing on it, by object identity. A lamp on furniture takes true-size
  edges. A vertical tube lamp tests 4 heights where the game tests its low end. Ceiling lamps keep their soft furniture shadows.
- **Openings.** Diagonal cut posts, diagonal windows and diagonal doors open to lamp light. Doors, arches and windows take the
  shape of the game's cutout masks, 822 shapes from 1,489 masks ([level-light-share](../features/night-lighting/level-light-share.md)).
- **Double-height rooms.** Room 0 of a story takes the lamps of the story below through their open tops, so the upper half of
  the walls is lit.
- **Outdoor lamp shadows.** The blocker search walks the rim of the ball's cone and a step counts only where it crosses a
  surface, so the penumbra stays whole at door jambs ([rt-outdoor-lamps](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md)).
- **Furniture light.** Furniture takes up to twice the lamp light at full night, clamped so it never burns white
  ([objects-and-rigs](../features/night-lighting/objects-and-rigs.md)).
- **Curtains and furniture along walls.** The set-in-a-wall test fired for almost every piece along a wall; these pieces now
  read a masked copy of the smooth room light, and doors keep the rig.
- **Arches.** The floor's arch blend takes the lot position from the floor vertex shader's TEXCOORD4: on denser maps the map uv put
  it at 2 or 4 times its place and it never reached a doorway. A lamp of another room is tested against the furniture of the rooms joined by an
  arch, so a shadow runs through the doorway; a room's own lamps walk the same records as before. The blend is no longer a fixed
  1.5 m: it grows with the rooms' contrast, up to 1.25 times the opening and 4 m, and the bounce takes the same reach. On a 2.66 m
  arch it runs 3.33 m, and the steepest fall per 25 cm goes from 26.5% to 11.3%.
- **Tone.** The step of the game's tone at mean 0.01 is spread over means 0.004 to 0.02 on GPU-drawn rooms, so a brighter texel no
  longer shows darker than its neighbour.
- **Glass.** The GPU path now charges the window's pass for each pane between a lamp and a texel, after the soft cut, on the
  directional maps too. Light through two panes was 1.71 times too strong.
- **Dawn and dusk.** Outdoor lamp light follows the sun's part of the game's lamp curve and moves at most its full range in 8 s of
  real time. It used to fall to its daytime 3% in under 0.6 s at dawn ([rt-outdoor-lamps](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md)).
- **Objects moved in a drag.** The room's blocker table is read again at most every 100 ms, and in every frame an object moved once
  a record of the room has moved, so an object's shadow follows it in place of waiting for the room's solve ([gpu-room-lamps](../features/night-lighting/gpu-room-lamps.md)).
- **Box-type sofas and chairs.** The GPU's copy takes short arms and a level cap, so the arms' sheets draw no thin streak past the seat.
- **Shader coverage.** Vertex shaders that write their free texture coordinate themselves take the Banding Fix grain, and 13 of 25
  refused glossy indoor furniture shaders take the smooth room light. A refusal logs the shader's hash.
- **Built-in shaders.** The cache holds all 105 shaders Apex registers, where the old file matched 50.
- **Defaults.** Ground light 0.8966; Objects, Seamless light and Fence brightness 1.0; Walls 0.56; Roofs 0.6; Reflection 1.0; the
  Natural lamp strength 0.2496; lot lamps with their own colour. Ambient Occlusion, Depth Blur and Edge Smoothing take new values, with
  FXAA as the edge method. The three built-in presets carry the same look. The Natural style's ground light is 0.8966.
- **Menu.** The Lamp lighting description and the Instant (GPU) status cover indoor and outdoor lamps, in all 21 languages.
- **Recording.** Up to 60 seconds. The stop key waits 8 s for the light to settle, a second press saves at once, and
  `Recording.txt` and the CSV carry more lines ([recorder](../features/dev-tools/recorder.md)).

- **Day and night switches.** Rooms that share a stairwell's light were refused their cached day or night solve; Apex sends them
  for a solve when the switch starts. A lamp edit's settle no longer solves rooms already solved since the switch, and a room
  whose cached solve a lamp edit dropped is sent at the switch. On a recorded Build mode switch the last room goes from +15.53 s
  to +2.14 s ([gpu-room-lamps](../features/night-lighting/gpu-room-lamps.md)).
- **Rooms solved again.** Their four GPU maps are built on worker threads while the previous solve keeps drawing, and uploads
  stop for the frame past 2 ms: one room of 47,681 texels held the render thread 76 ms. In game, 119 maps took 813 ms on workers,
  with the largest upload frame at 7.49 ms.
- **Attics and rooms open to the roof.** A room counts as open above from the upper story's own walls, rooms bounded by diagonal
  walls are found open, and the attic's walls take the interior wall's response, the room's window light and its night level.
- **Lamps of other lots.** A lamp the game switches on another lot is no player's edit: its marks go at the game's pace, and the
  world-wide rig regather after a lamp refresh is kept for the lot being played.

## Fixes

- Under the RTX Remix bridge the window stayed black: `GetBackBuffer` and render target 0 were two objects. Apex's own calls get
  render target 0 (issue 59).
- With Light detail off, a layout check copied from address 8 on every room solve; memory reads below 0x10000 are refused (issue 55).
- Lamp light no longer leaks through a floor slab: points on the floor line are moved 12 cm to their own side of it.

- A story map scan walked four map classes where a story manager has three, which raised 250 to 1,000 handled access violations
  a second on the main thread (issue 55).
- A model left past the scene budget with a shorter LOD could be drawn past its part array (issue 56)
  ([scene-node-budget](../features/performance/scene-node-budget.md)).
- Household photos keep the hair colour of blond Sims.
- A table's shadow no longer flickers in a drag. At a read every 100 ms, a table dragged with its lamp stood up to 0.9 m behind the
  lamp, its floor shadow swung by 2.3 times that and its fill went off ([gpu-room-lamps](../features/night-lighting/gpu-room-lamps.md)).
- The dark 1 m diamonds beside a diagonal arch are gone: on a tile kept in the map's split atlas, the map uv held an address over
  20 m away and the tile drew with no arch blend ([arches](../features/night-lighting/arches.md)).
- A deck beside a diagonal wall shows a lamp's light with no steps. A whole room-0 tile there keeps its texels in the split atlas
  while outdoor floors read the map by position, so those texels are now relit at the tile's place too
  ([room-light-maps](../engine/room-light-maps.md)).
- A furniture shadow ends in a 0.12 m fade, where the game's record sphere cut it in a crisp round arc: on a recorded scene the step
  goes from 0.80 to 0.16, and ceiling lamps keep their soft shadows ([gpu-room-lamps](../features/night-lighting/gpu-room-lamps.md)).
- Street-class lamps and sconces reach the game's brightness up close: the street factor and the wall lamp's raise follow the cap.
- A lamp's own parts are no longer lit by its bulb on rigs of other rooms.
- Sharpening no longer multiplies the Banding Fix grain on flat areas ([edge-smoothing](../features/edge-smoothing.md)).
- Timing stamps written 1 ms ahead no longer read as expired, at 35 sites of the lamp, hold and wait code.
- In a long session, a map, a shader object or a room 0 that failed once tries again on a timer instead of staying dead until a
  solve, a lot change or a device reset; the memory budget flag clears when memory comes back; 128 maps are watched, up from 64.

## Known limitations

- GPU room lamps need Steam 1.67.2 or EA app 1.69.47; other game builds keep the game's room solves indoors and Instant outdoors.
- The RTX Remix bridge: on the EA app both `bridge.conf` files need `client.targetProcess = TS3.exe`; Sims3SettingsSetter's
  borderless window does not apply under it.
- Basement lamps keep the game's solves; a lamp crossing into room 0 takes the solves for its next edits.
- Opening shapes and table legs are baked from the game's packages: custom content keeps its kind's shape and the table's top.
- Shadows and bounce pass arches only, not doors, windows or a second room; past the blend's reach, 1.5 to 4 m, each room keeps its own
  ambient, and rooms sharing an ambient stay at 1.5 m.
- Table legs cast no shadow from lamps 3 m or more over their tops, and a faint one past 1.5 m.
- The end fade covers furniture bands only: true-size edges and table legs keep the game's end at the sphere.
- A deck keeps its steps on half tiles, on maps with no relit copy and on maps with kept parts.
- Room 0 and the directional maps wait for the solve for a moved object; glass is charged on kept rooms only.
- Object rigs and tree lamps still turn at the sky's moment at dawn.
- Shaders with no lamp chain stay refused: 168 outdoor object shaders and smaller groups.
- Open checks are on the [validation page](../validation/night-lighting-gpu-room-lamps.md).

## Upgrade notes

- The lighting defaults move once, at the first start (`[meta] lighting_defaults`), and only where a key still holds its old default;
  the three lamp colour keys move together or not at all. A value chosen on purpose that equals the old default moves too.
- A player who picked the old Natural style shows Custom and keeps the ground light of 1.67.
- Ambient Occlusion, Depth Blur and Edge Smoothing keep the values of an existing config; the new ones reach new installs and presets.
- [architecture.md](../architecture.md) has the rule.
