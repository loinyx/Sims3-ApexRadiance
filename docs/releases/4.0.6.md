# Apex Radiance 4.0.6

2026-11-18 · [v4.0.6](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v4.0.6) · ApexRadianceSetup.exe, Apex-Radiance-4.0.6-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**Potted plants cast leaf-shaped shadows.** A lamp past a potted plant used to cast a wedge with straight edges: the
game's blocker for a plant is two flat sheets crossed in an X. Plants now shade lamp light with soft balls baked from
their own mesh, with a solid pot under the leaves, and custom content plants are baked when the game starts.

## Changes

- **Plant shadows** ([GPU room lamps, 35](../features/night-lighting/gpu-room-lamps.md)): 87 of the game's models (plants by
  their blockers or their name) baked into 725 balls (`tools/plant_puffs_table`); the mesh's thick pieces (a pot, a box)
  block like the game's blockers, the leaves let light through by their area. The balls stand in for a record's upright
  sheets; a level blocker (a pot's top) stays.
- **Custom content plants.** `Mods\**\*.package` and `DCCache\*.dbc` are baked by the same steps on a background thread
  when the game starts (about 1 s for 106 files on the test PC); the C++ bake gives the script's balls exactly on the
  game's packages.

## Known limitations

- No leaf texture inside a ball: the plant's shadow is soft lobes, not single leaves.
- Custom content installed while the game runs is baked at the next start; models without a shadow LOD keep the
  crossed sheets.
- Other objects with the crossed sheets (a hamper, showers, a decorative rocket) take their own shape too, unchecked in
  game.

## Upgrade notes

- No setting moves.
