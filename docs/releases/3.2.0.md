# Apex Radiance 3.2.0

2026-11-13 · [v3.2.0](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v3.2.0) · ApexRadianceSetup.exe, Apex-Radiance-3.2.0-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**Faster instant lighting, higher FPS, and no more black lot ground with the RTX Remix bridge.** Instant (GPU) light
reaches a lot, a floor switch and a lamp edit in a fraction of the time it took in 3.1.6, and the median FPS on the
standard night route went from 90 to 113. A crash after deleting lamps and lots whose ground drew black under the RTX
Remix bridge are fixed.

## Changes

- **Arriving at a lot.** The GPU light shows on a lot within 0.8 s even with the camera moving (was a fixed 3 s hold);
  for 8 s after a lot switch the ground story goes first and every story's room 0 is queued at once (the gym fully on
  GPU light in 4.2 to 4.3 s instead of 10.6 to 11.5 s); a room-0 solve that kept no parts is asked again after 1.5 s
  instead of 10 s ([real-time outdoor lamps](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md)).
- **Lots left.** The lot left keeps its relit maps until the new lot is relit, and up to three lots left keep the GPU
  light until the game writes their maps again, instead of falling back to the game's older maps
  ([real-time outdoor lamps](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md)).
- **Lamp edits.** Median 1.7 s to 0.34 to 0.78 s, worst 8.8 s to 5.3 s on the standard route; the pool light keeps its
  link to the rooms it lights (off 5.8 s to 0.09 to 0.45 s, colour 8.8 s to 0.75 s); a story already covered takes the
  edit while another waits; dragged lamps keep their rooms urgent for the whole drag.
- **Floor switches.** Window cut records are keyed by cell, so a window the game posts again changes nothing (9 light
  recomputes per test to 0); the light registry refilled at a switch is no longer read as lamp edits (29 phantom edits
  per minute to 3).
- **Performance.** Median FPS on the standard night route 90 to 113; fast camera hitch rate 6.3 to 6.7% to 3.0%, worst
  frame 101 to 111 ms to 83 to 93 ms; room-0 solves of the lots around the active one wait for the camera to stop
  (2 s at most); foliage keeps its lamp picks while indoor lamps shine out (29 to 14 us a draw).
- **Light capture (developer).** A light capture compares each small texture of the draws with what the GPU samples,
  and development builds check lot terrain paint and overlay maps as the game binds them
  ([light probe](../features/dev-tools/light-probe.md)).

## Fixes

- **Crash after deleting lamps** ([#76](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/issues/76)). OutdoorDelta reads a
  lamp's position only while the lamp's table still points to the game's position function, and the light class
  tables stay read-only ([outdoor lamps](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md)).
- **Black lot ground under the RTX Remix bridge.** The bridge client left a new texture level's CPU copy under 64 KB
  uninitialized; the game wrote part of a lot's terrain overlay weights (64x64) and uploaded the rest, so some lots drew
  their ground black on some loads. Bridge patch 0014 starts the copy zeroed, as d3d9 and DXVK do: on the affected park
  lot the ground was black on 4 of 4 loads before and right on 4 of 4 after ([RTX Remix bridge](../features/rtx-remix-bridge.md)).

## Known limitations

- The gains are measured with the in-game runner on Steam 1.67.2 at night; the EA app 1.69 build is unverified.
- In a few lot maps the light capture's GPU readback differs from the game's copy in about 250 texels; the cause is
  not known and nothing on screen shows it.
