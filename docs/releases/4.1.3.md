# Apex Radiance 4.1.3

Released 2026-11-26 · ApexLauncher.exe (also attached as ApexRadianceSetup.exe for older Setups' update), Apex-Radiance-4.1.3-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**Apex Launcher 2.3.2 and one structure for every menu page.** The game's caches get their own page in Saves, custom content inside a
Sims3Pack goes in as mods, and the colour theme chosen in the launcher now colours Apex Radiance's menu in the game,
which had a design pass over every page.

## Changes

- **Game caches** ([saves-and-backups.md](../features/launcher/saves-and-backups.md#game-caches)): a place in Saves with
  every cache group as a row (size and switch), grouped by when to clear it and by disk space, and its files to keep
  one by one; half-written content files and backups of installed content can be cleared too. Health's cache action
  opens it; the dialog in Mods is gone.
- **Clean a save** ([saves-and-backups.md](../features/launcher/saves-and-backups.md#cleaning)): each part on its own
  row with its size, *Empty* when there is nothing to take, and copies of the save kept inside its folder.
- **Sims3Packs** ([mods.md](../features/launcher/mods.md#adding-files)): Add mods, drag and drop and *Open with* take
  `.sims3pack` files; the custom content in one comes out as mods named after their items, store items included. Lots,
  worlds and Sims still go through the game's own launcher.
- **Load order** ([load-order-and-conflicts.md](../features/launcher/load-order-and-conflicts.md)): mods are reordered by
  dragging, the grip always shown, on a page polished to the design system.
- **Colour theme in the game** ([settings.md](../features/launcher/settings.md), [ui.md](../ui.md)): the launcher writes
  its theme to `[ui] theme_*`; the menu takes its accent and the main button's gradient from it, on the launcher's
  neutral greys.
- **Menu design pass** ([ui.md](../ui.md)): the sidebar is as wide as its longest name; every dropdown, list and set of
  steps uses one shared look; slider tracks end at the same place; Text size has icon buttons; Ambient Occlusion's
  Advanced comes last; Filters and LUTs have their own icons.
- **Menu navigation** ([ui.md](../ui.md#page-structure-every-page-follows-it)): every feature page has its switch, GPU
  cost and compare tools on the title line (`ApexUi::PageHeader`) and stays readable, greyed, while off, with no
  per-tab "turn it on" notes. The sidebar groups are World (Lighting, with Water & Snow as a tab), Image (the picture
  effects), System (Display, Performance with Lot streaming as a tab) and Tools (Capture, Report a problem, Settings,
  Developer); the Attention page became cards on Overview. Overview's Effects card uses the shared row, in sidebar order;
  search results follow the sidebar; Refresh lights has one name everywhere; Shaders is out of beta.
- **Dialogs and components** ([ui.md](../ui.md)): every dialog through `ApexUi::BeginModal` (440 or 560 units, a card's
  padding and corners, opaque popups); notes share one height and padding; in-card searches use one search field;
  disabled rows are easier to read; numbers use the language's decimal mark.
- **Merge dialog** ([mod-tools.md](../features/launcher/mod-tools.md)): Add mods to a merge lists the merge's own folder first,
  with All mods beside it, Select all and Ctrl+A; dialogs leave room for a field's right border.
- **Home** ([home-and-play.md](../features/launcher/home-and-play.md)): the picture keeps its own shape and height.
- **Add mods dialog** ([mods.md](../features/launcher/mods.md#adding-files)): rows joined into one card, ticked by a
  click anywhere on them, with the files left out as rows with their reason.

## Fixes

- The game no longer crashes when the RTX Remix bridge passes a vertex format pointer below 64 KB.
- Game settings read a `GraphicsRules.sgr` edited by hand (shadow resolution showed blank).
- Colours that kept the old theme after a switch between light and dark follow it (Mods' list or grid switch, the
  list's boxes, sizes and arrows).
- The caches' file lists no longer scroll inside the page; Health tiles keep their own hover colour.

## Upgrade notes

The launcher updates itself; the mod is updated from the launcher's Components page or by the manual zip.
