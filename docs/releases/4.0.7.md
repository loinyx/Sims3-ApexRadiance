# Apex Radiance 4.0.7

2026-11-19 · [v4.0.7](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v4.0.7) · ApexRadianceSetup.exe, Apex-Radiance-4.0.7-Manual-install.zip

## Highlights

**Lamp light stays behind walls and follows every edit at once.** Three places where a lamp still got past a wall are
closed (a wall's end, the floor beside it, the ground under a roofed room), and room 0's walls of every story, half walls
included, take a lamp's switch or drag in the same frame. Contributions by @rodneypantonial (Sims 3 Modernization) add
performance and Ambient Occlusion options, on by default, RTX Remix bridge fixes and Setup's experimental channel.

## New features

- **Fewer mouse picks when still** ([pick-skip-when-still.md](../features/performance/pick-skip-when-still.md)) and
  **Frame deadline while moving** ([deadline-slicing.md](../features/performance/deadline-slicing.md)), on by default;
  the **shared room solve time** of Lot Lighting While Moving, on by default (configuration file only).
- **Ambient Occlusion:** Fewer directions when far, Steady direction count, Fewer directions in the open, Smooth flat
  surfaces, Edge-aware smoothing and Refine while the camera is still (Quality Low), on by default under *Advanced*
  ([ambient-occlusion.md](../features/ambient-occlusion.md)).
- **Setup 1.2.7:** Components > Sims 3 Modernization offers a Stable or Experimental channel.

## Changes

- **Switched-on lamps** ([rt-outdoor-lamps.md](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md)): a lit lamp of the lot
  that no room-0 list holds (switched on: while off every story gathered again without it) is drawn on every story where
  it is drawable at once; before, its walls waited 3 to 10 s for each story's solve.
- **Half walls of a top story:** a map whose own lamps changed during a lamp edit goes past the 2 ms CPU budget, within
  the GPU budget; a story with room 0 alone was never urgent nor edited, and showed a dragged lamp up to a second late.
- **RTX Remix bridge:** the same card id on every start (the server reads `dxvk.conf` beside the client), device reset on
  a DXVK server, state blocks keep the vertex input, idle copy fixes.

## Fixes

- A wall crossed 5 to 7.6 cm before a lit point blocks the lamp (wall ends and T joints).
- Floor points within 7 cm of a wall line read the shadow 7 cm off it on their own side (no band beyond the wall).
- Room 0's ground under a roofed room takes no GPU lamp, and the lot terrain reads the room light map where the floors do;
  outside walls under a roofed room keep their GPU lamps.
- A start-up deadlock between the hook registration and PostScene locks; no routing error with Sims 3 Modernization.

## Known limitations

- A lamp switch right after a world load still waits for the after-load refresh's room-0 solves on the CPU side; the
  walls already show the GPU light.
- The bridge fixes and Setup's channel were not run in game by the maintainer.

## Upgrade notes

- No setting moves; the new options take their defaults in existing configurations.
