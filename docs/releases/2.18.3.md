# Apex Radiance 2.18.3

2026-10-30 · [v2.18.3](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.18.3) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

**Hotfix: lamp light on the ground changed with the camera.** The lamps' light on sidewalks, on the grass by the lots
and on a lot's ground could change or go dark as the camera moved. It now stays the same from every angle, and a
wall's shadow on the grass no longer stops in a straight line at a lot's edge.

## Fixes

- Sidewalks keep the street and garden lamps' light from every camera angle: their terrain shader was taken or left to
  the game depending on a constant left by an earlier draw
  ([world-atlas-and-smoothed-maps](../features/night-lighting/world-atlas-and-smoothed-maps.md#registration-recordworldchunk-render-thread-per-draw)).
- The grass by the lots and the lot ground keep the same lamp strength as the camera moves: each terrain chunk uses one
  light map, the one the game rebuilds with the current lamps, on every terrain variant.
- The smoothed ground light is no longer rebuilt while the camera moves (about 50 times a second before, in a
  recording).
- Objects show their light without delay when the camera moves between lots without lamp posts: those lots no longer
  pick neighbours, whose rooms were solved again at every change
  ([rt-outdoor-lamps](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md)).

- A wall's shadow on the grass continues past a lot's edge with no straight cut: the world grass took the walls' shade
  averaged with lots next door that see the same lamp but not the wall
  ([lot-light-pass](../features/night-lighting/lot-light-pass.md)).

## Known limitations

- Tested in game at night in the rain; dry weather and snow use the same code but were not checked in game for this
  release.
- Right after a world loads, the ground shows the lamps without the walls' shade for a few seconds, until each lot's
  shade is built.
