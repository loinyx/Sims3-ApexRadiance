# Apex Radiance 1.5.1

Published 2026-09-29 · tag [`v1.5.1`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v1.5.1) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Steadier night lighting and smooth light indoors.

## New features

- **Smooth indoor light (Experimental):** stairs, curtains and furniture inside the house now take the room's light smoothly, so one side of a staircase or one curtain is no longer darker than the one next to it. You can turn it off in Lighting > Objects > Indoors.

## Changes

- **Faster lamp updates:** switching a lot's lamps off or on now updates the light inside and outside the house within a few seconds, without going into Build mode.

## Fixes

- **Lamp light stays put when you move the camera:** at some camera angles the fixed lamp light on the ground could vanish for a moment and come back. It now stays on at every angle.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
