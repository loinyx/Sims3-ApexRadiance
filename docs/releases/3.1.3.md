# Apex Radiance 3.1.3

2026-11-12 · [v3.1.3](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v3.1.3) · ApexRadiance.asi, ApexRadianceSetup.exe

## Highlights

**A smooth camera when zoomed out, and steadier lamp light at night.** Zooming out and the map view no longer drop the
frame rate: Apex's fence and stair lighting cost about 36 ms a frame there and now costs about 1 ms. Doors and objects
beside coloured lamps, and the upper walls of double-height rooms, now take the light around them.

## Changes

- **Fences and stairs, zoomed out.** A fence or stair group more than 60 m from the camera keeps the pieces and
  positions read last at its place for 2 s instead of locking the game's instance buffer at every draw, which waited
  for the graphics card: 36 ms to 1.2 ms a frame zoomed out (17 to 80 fps measured)
  ([lot light pass](../features/night-lighting/lot-light-pass.md)).
- **Fences and stairs, close up.** The game's writes to the instance buffers fence draws read are copied when it
  finishes writing them, and the draw reads the copy, the same bytes, without waiting: 9.5 ms to 0.2 ms a frame close
  up, about 5 us a draw ([lot light pass](../features/night-lighting/lot-light-pass.md)).
- **Active lot during a pan.** An active lot still preparing its relit maps holds while the camera moves (for up to
  3 s), instead of switching up to 4 times in 750 ms and dropping its maps at each switch
  ([real-time outdoor lamps](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md#active-lot-switch-while-the-camera-moves)).

- **Restart host (experimental).** `ts3wrap.exe` is in the release and Apex Radiance Setup installs it with Apex
  Radiance. It stays off until `[ui] restart_host = true` in `ApexRadiance.toml`: after a save that leaves little free
  address space, quitting the game asks whether to start it again into that save
  ([restart host](../features/performance/restart-host.md)).

## Fixes

- **Objects near a lamp.** The directional maps doors, switches and other objects are lit from take every lamp's part
  through the lamp law walls and floors use (`basis_lamp_law`): 3 m from a red shaded lamp, a door went from full red
  (255) to the level of the wall beside it ([light between stories](../features/night-lighting/level-light-share.md)).
- **Double-height rooms.** The upper half of a double-height room's wall keeps the room's ambient where no lamp
  reaches it, instead of going black beside the same room's grey ([walls](../features/night-lighting/walls.md)).
- **Bone index guard.** A Sim wearing a custom part whose mesh names a bone its bone list does not have no longer
  crashes the game while the Sim is built ([bone index guard](../features/bone-index-guard.md)).

## Known limitations

- The fence copy and the far-group keep are measured in one session on Steam 1.67.2; the EA app 1.69 build is
  unverified.
- The bone index guard's patterns were checked on Steam 1.67.2; on EA app 1.69 it looks for them at start-up and logs which
  one it found (or that it stays off).
- When the active lot does switch, the new lot shows the game's maps for 3 to 5 s before its relit maps.
