# Apex Radiance 3.1.2

2026-11-12 · [v3.1.2](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v3.1.2) · ApexRadiance.asi, ApexRadianceSetup.exe

## Highlights

**Smoother camera, and lamp light that stays put around town.** Lots the camera pans past keep the glow of their lamps
on the lawn, and stairs and fences hold a steady light. In Frame Profiler runs under the RTX Remix bridge, the slowest
5 % of frames went from 71 to 35 ms and hitch frames from 9 % to 4.4 %.

## Changes

- **Lots leaving full detail.** When the game's lot detail takes a lot out of full detail, its lamps stay in the
  ground light instead of going dark and being taken as a lamp switch-off; they leave it on any real change, 30 s after
  the lot is detailed again, in Build mode or at dawn. Steam 1.67.2 only
  ([terrain relight](../features/night-lighting/terrain-relight.md)).
- **Active-lot switch.** A switch of the active lot is no longer a lamp edit: the regathered rooms keep their place in
  the queue with quiet marks, without the lamp-edit budgets that ran for about 8 s while the camera moved (moving and
  loading frames at 28 ms against 15 ms in a capture)
  ([light between stories](../features/night-lighting/level-light-share.md#lamp-posts-of-other-lots)).
- **Ground room first.** For up to 1 s after an active-lot switch, the new lot's ground room is solved first, so the
  glow under its lamp posts shows sooner (3.5 s late in a capture before)
  ([faster room lighting](../features/performance/room-light-queue.md#ground-first-after-an-active-lot-switch)).
- **Active lot hold.** The active lot stays while the camera moves and until it has been still for 300 ms, instead of
  switching back and forth and rebuilding the outdoor lamp maps. The lot just left keeps its room-0 records for 20 s,
  so a return reuses them, and the base-only records of lots next to the active one have their own 16 MB budget
  ([real-time outdoor lamps](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md#active-lot-switch-while-the-camera-moves)).
- **Fences and stairs.** A fence or stair draw costs 7 to 11 us instead of 386 us: fence groups are kept by their
  pieces, not by their place in the game's refilled buffers, and the fence grid is built at most once a frame, its gaps
  and the lots' wall shade only when the walls change ([lot light pass](../features/night-lighting/lot-light-pass.md)).
- **Instant (GPU).** On a lot with more lamps than one GPU batch, the floor's lamp sums are computed on a worker thread
  instead of in 105 to 255 ms render-thread hitches; the floor keeps its light until they arrive
  ([real-time outdoor lamps](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md)).
- **Moving between lots.** Lots streaming in and the lot just left no longer count as lamp edits: their rooms are solved
  at the game's pace instead of in 8 s of urgent room solves (about 10 ms a frame); after a real lamp edit they stay
  urgent ([light between stories](../features/night-lighting/level-light-share.md#lamp-posts-of-other-lots)).
- **Lighting draws.** The fixes for furniture, outdoor objects, fences and stairs set their pixel shader constants as
  one register run and only the sampler states that differ, about 1000 draws a frame; the image is unchanged
  ([Night Lighting](../features/night-lighting/README.md#per-draw-lot_light_bridgecpp)).
- **Lot Streaming.** *Smooth lot streaming*, *Spread lot objects while loading* and *Maximum detailed lots* are
  removed: Smooth lot streaming brought lots in one at a time, and the game's own option for how many lots are detailed
  now always applies.
  *Extended lot detail* keeps its distance; *Keep lot visibility stable* and *Pause lot streaming in map view* stay.
  The removed options' saved settings are ignored. *Keep lot visibility stable* ranks a lot already in full detail at
  0.5 of its squared distance instead of 0.81, so lots switch detail less while the camera moves
  ([lot streaming](../features/performance/lot-streaming.md)).
- **Spread lot lighting while moving.** Lots loading while the camera moves keep at least 3/15 of the game's lighting
  time (6 or 2 ms), so lots requested while panning finish without waiting for the camera to stop
  ([lot lighting while moving](../features/performance/lot-lighting-motion.md)).
- **Apex Radiance Setup.** A first install opens on a welcome page, and updates of a game with Apex Radiance show in
  their own card. Each renderer is a card with its description, the installed one marked. The RTX Remix bridge is
  labelled experimental and offered only when a graphics card has Vulkan 1.3 ([installer](../features/installer.md)).
- **Log.** The log writer no longer holds its lock while writing, so a line logged on the render thread does not wait
  for the disk, and the previous session's log is kept as `ApexRadiance_LOG.old.txt`
  ([logger](../architecture.md#6-logger-and-crash-report)).
- **Recordings.** Recordings over water follow the plain camera, not the reflection's mirrored copy, so their ground
  points find the lamp ([recorder](../features/dev-tools/recorder.md)).
- **Developer mode.** *Record story light samples* is no longer saved: left on, it kept the lamp visibility memos off
  in every later session (outdoor objects at 20 ms a frame). The Frame Profiler's measurement presets time every draw
  hook and turn per-hook timing on ([frame profiler](../features/frame-profiler.md)).

## Fixes

- **Stairs flickering as the camera moved.** The game refills its instance buffers, so a stair's pieces took another
  group's lamps until the next read; each group is now found by its pieces' origins at every draw
  ([fences](../features/night-lighting/fences.md)).
- **Steps of different brightness along a stair.** Each stair and fence piece takes its own lamp shares and ground
  light; pieces on a foundation's wall line are tested from the lamp's side, and pieces within 1 m of the lot's ground
  take the lamps they see whole under the lot's ground lamp shade.
- **Lot options turned off in a loaded world.** Turning lot options off mid-world no longer leaves 4 detailed lots and
  frozen streaming: Apex no longer writes the number of detailed lots or the camera speed at which lots stop streaming,
  so the game's own option stays in effect ([removed features](../removed-features.md#lot-streaming-parts)).
- **Shine on hair under the RTX Remix bridge.** The bridge's `dxvk.conf` no longer forces 16x anisotropic filtering,
  which spoiled the specular of hair and other glossy textures; filtering follows the game's and the driver's settings
  (thanks swiffyjk, [#66](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/issues/66);
  [RTX Remix bridge](../features/rtx-remix-bridge.md)).
- **Setup with the game running.** Setup reads the Modernization log (`ts3native.log`) while the game keeps it open,
  instead of failing on every refresh ([installer](../features/installer.md)).

## Known limitations

- Steady stairs are seen in play on a development build but not yet confirmed with an F7 capture, and mesh fences and
  gates are not yet checked after the change of fence groups
  ([validation](../validation/night-lighting-fences.md#open-checks)).
- On a lot with more lamps than one GPU batch, a floor waits up to 2 s for the worker's lamp sums; the wait at a lot's
  first build and a lamp post edit next to another lot are not yet checked in game
  ([validation](../validation/night-lighting-level-light-share.md#open-checks)).
- Lot Streaming without the removed options, and turning its switches off in a loaded world, are not yet checked in
  game ([validation](../validation/performance-lot-streaming.md#open-checks)).
- The lamps kept on the ground of a lot leaving full detail (the LOD hold) are not yet checked in game
  ([validation](../validation/night-lighting-terrain-relight.md#open-checks)).
- The ground room first after an active-lot switch is not yet checked in game
  ([validation](../validation/performance-room-light-queue.md#open-checks)).
- The active lot hold in public builds and the 20 s grace for the lot just left are not yet checked in game
  ([validation](../validation/night-lighting-level-light-share.md#open-checks)).
