# Apex Radiance 3.1.2

2026-11-11 · [v3.1.2](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v3.1.2) · ApexRadiance.asi, ApexRadianceSetup.exe

## Highlights

**Smoother camera on busy lots, and stairs and fences keep a steady light.** Outdoor stairs no longer flicker as the
camera moves, and their steps no longer show blocks of different brightness. In Frame Profiler runs under the RTX Remix
bridge, the slowest 5 % of frames went from 71 to 35 ms and hitch frames from 9 % to 4.4 %.

## Changes

- **Fences and stairs.** A fence or stair draw costs 7 to 11 us instead of 386 us: fence groups are kept by their
  pieces, not by their place in the game's refilled buffers, and the fence grid is built at most once a frame, its gaps
  and the lots' wall shade only when the walls change ([lot light pass](../features/night-lighting/lot-light-pass.md)).
- **Instant (GPU).** On a lot with more lamps than one GPU batch, the floor's lamp sums are computed on a worker thread
  instead of in 105 to 255 ms render-thread hitches; the floor keeps its light until they arrive
  ([real-time outdoor lamps](../features/night-lighting/rt-outdoor-lamps.md)).
- **Moving between lots.** Lots streaming in and the lot just left no longer count as lamp edits: their rooms are solved
  at the game's pace instead of in 8 s of urgent room solves (about 10 ms a frame); after a real lamp edit they stay
  urgent ([light between stories](../features/night-lighting/level-light-share.md#lamp-posts-of-other-lots)).
- **Lighting draws.** The fixes for furniture, outdoor objects, fences and stairs set their pixel shader constants as
  one register run and only the sampler states that differ, about 1000 draws a frame; the image is unchanged
  ([Night Lighting](../features/night-lighting/README.md#per-draw-lot_light_bridgecpp)).
- **Lot Streaming.** *Smooth lot streaming*, *Spread lot objects while loading* and *Maximum detailed lots* are
  removed: Smooth lot streaming brought lots in one at a time, and the game's own option for how many lots are detailed
  now always applies.
  *Extended lot detail* keeps its distance; *Keep lot visibility stable* and *Pause lot streaming in map view* stay.
  The removed options' saved settings are ignored ([lot streaming](../features/performance/lot-streaming.md)).
- **Spread lot lighting while moving.** Lots loading while the camera moves keep at least 3/15 of the game's lighting
  time (6 or 2 ms), so lots requested while panning finish without waiting for the camera to stop
  ([lot lighting while moving](../features/performance/lot-lighting-motion.md)).
- **Apex Radiance Setup.** The RTX Remix bridge renderer is labelled experimental ([installer](../features/installer.md)).
- **Developer mode.** *Record story light samples* is no longer saved: left on, it kept the lamp visibility memos off
  in every later session (outdoor objects at 20 ms a frame). The Frame Profiler's measurement presets time every draw
  hook and turn per-hook timing on ([frame profiler](../features/frame-profiler.md)).

## Fixes

- **Stairs flickering as the camera moved.** The game refills its instance buffers, so a stair's pieces took another
  group's lamps until the next read; each group is now found by its pieces' origins at every draw
  ([fences](../features/night-lighting/fences.md)).
- **Steps of different brightness along a stair.** Each stair and fence piece takes its own lamp shares and ground
  light; pieces on a foundation's wall line are tested from the lamp's side, and pieces within 1 m of the lot's ground
  take the lamps they see whole under the lot's ground lamp shade.
- **Lot options turned off in a loaded world.** Turning lot options off mid-world no longer leaves 4 detailed lots and
  frozen streaming: Apex no longer writes the number of detailed lots or the camera speed at which lots stop streaming,
  so the game's own option stays in effect ([removed features](../removed-features.md#lot-streaming-parts)).

## Known limitations

- Steady stairs are seen in play on a development build but not yet confirmed with an F7 capture, and mesh fences and
  gates are not yet checked after the change of fence groups
  ([validation](../validation/night-lighting-fences.md#open-checks)).
- On a lot with more lamps than one GPU batch, a floor waits up to 2 s for the worker's lamp sums; the wait at a lot's
  first build and a lamp post edit next to another lot are not yet checked in game
  ([validation](../validation/night-lighting-level-light-share.md#open-checks)).
- Lot Streaming without the removed options, and turning its switches off in a loaded world, are not yet checked in
  game ([validation](../validation/performance-lot-streaming.md#open-checks)).
