### WP6: Ambient Occlusion with the scene's normals

`gbufferNormals = true` (and `[patches.GBuffer]` on): where target 2 holds a normal (roughness above 0), `GtaoPS` decodes
it (octahedral), rotates it into its view space with the camera's axes and uses it instead of the normal from depth. The
axes come from the view-projection rows: x = row 0, y = row 1, z = row 3, each over its length (`GBufferMotion::ViewRows`;
exact for a centred projection, which the game uses: jitter is off). The normal is turned to face the camera as before.
Pixels without a written normal (interior floors, the shaders without a probe read) keep the depth normal. The
isolated-pixel fade and the march still use the depth.

### WP7: temporal core

`temporal = true`: after the four blur passes, `TemporalPS` writes a history target (`A16B16G16R16F`: shade, 1/z, material
id, frames) and the composite reads it instead of the blurred shade.

- **Reprojection:** the previous position from the G-buffer's motion target (`uv - mv · (0.5, -0.5)`); camera motion only
  until WP8 adds movers.
- **Rejection** (a new history, this frame's value): history not written in the frame before (a skipped frame, a new
  target), invalid motion (camera cut, no last camera), off screen, motion class 3 (Sims, foliage, water: the spatial
  filter alone, research 14 §2c), another material id, or a 1/z that the camera does not explain: the expected previous
  1/z is `(1/z) / q.w` with `q.w = dot(D row 3, (ndc, d, 1))`, the ratio of the previous to this frame's view depth, and
  more than 5 % off starts again.
- **Clip and blend:** the history is clamped to the 3 × 3 neighbourhood's mean ± 1.5 σ, then blended with weight
  1 / frames, at most 8 frames.
- The shade itself is unchanged: Ambient Occlusion stays deterministic (no per-frame jitter), so a still camera gives the
  same value at once and nothing twinkles where the history restarts. The filter is the core for later temporal
  consumers (jittered AO, the TAA of WP12, GI accumulation); its value for today's shade is stability along edges in a
  pan, which the smoke shows or not.

### WP10: composited albedo

The rule, worked out on 18 disassembled shaders and checked over every composited shader:

- Each sampler gets a class from the texture names its passes bind: **albedo** (`OverlayTexture`, `DiffuseMap`,
  `colorMap..4`, `compositeMap`, `TerrainSnowDiffuseMap`, `RoadTexture`, `TwigDiffuseMap`), **material** (blend map,
  terrain detail, cutout, skin tone and detail maps, snow melt, leaf, blood and ghost noise maps) or **light** (every
  other name, unknown ones included).
- Per temp component, the instructions carry two marks: which albedo channels the value depends on, and whether a light
  texture or an `nrm` result went into it. The albedo is `rR.xyz` at the first component-wise instruction that reads it
  unswizzled, albedo-marked and light-free, beside a light-marked source: the place where the blended texture colour
  meets the light. Without such a place, an `oC0.xyz` write that is albedo-marked and light-free is an unlit pass (the
  terrain layer passes), and its colour is the albedo.
- The table stores the instruction index counted without `dcl` / `def` (unchanged by the Banding Fix copy, which adds
  only declarations and a tail) and the register; `AddGBufferPs` inserts one `mov` after it, or for the `oC0` case a
  copy of that instruction into the albedo temp just before it. Inside flow control: refused.
- **Sim skin:** `OverlayTexture` is the composited outfit and skin texture; the albedo is it times the material step
  (skin tone factor, or tint and tan blend), before the light. **Terrain:** the layer blend by `blendMap`, detail × 2 and
  the snow blend; the unlit layer passes give their `oC0` colour, which is premultiplied by the layer weight where
  `oC0.a < 1` (those passes are blended and therefore masked at the draw).
- The cheap alternatives, for comparison: `OverlayTexture` alone as the skin albedo misses the tone step (up to −15 % red,
  +8 % green and blue where the overlay alpha is 0, from the factor `(0.92 + 0.08 a, 1.04 − 0.04 a, 1.04 − 0.04 a)²`) and
  the tan blend; terrain layer 0 alone is wrong wherever another layer or snow has weight, most painted ground.

### Probe normals in the GB-03 families

Every probe-row shader of ExteriorFloors, Road, SingleObject, InstancedObject, Counters, Roof, Night, ExteriorWall
(`ps_3_0`) and Phong, Day, Road, ExteriorFloors, TerrainHigh, Roof, Counters, Landmark (`ps_2_0`) was traced from the probe
read back through the pixel shader and its paired vertex shader (4,669 shaders; two pairs per family read in full):

| Family | Version | Normal | Mark |
|---|---|---|---|
| ExteriorFloors | 3, 2 | the constant up vector, or a world-space normal map on the flat floor, normalised | **verified** |
| Road | 3, 2 | world-space normal map (`xz` from the map, `y` rebuilt), detail map added in world space | **verified** |
| SingleObject, InstancedObject | 3 | the instance's yaw, then the world rows that also move the position (`c8..c10` or `c80..c82`), normal map or not, normalised | **verified** |
| Counters | 3 | `c12..c14`, `c16..c18` or the skinned rows (the position's rows), normal map, normalised | **verified** |
| Roof, Night, ExteriorWall | 3 | the position's world rows, normal map where bound; a plain ExteriorWall normal is normalised in the vertex shader only | **verified** |
| Phong, Day, Roof, Landmark | 2 | the position's world rows, normal map where bound | **verified** |
| TerrainHigh | 2 | world-space normal map plus the snow normal map | **verified** |
| Counters (48 of 144) | 2 | **not the normal**: the probe sampler is read with the light-space position the vertex shader writes (`WorldToLightSpace` rows), in the shadow variants | **refuted**: probe dropped |
| leafMesh, leafCard, frond, branchMain, Foliage, particles (102) | 2 | the coordinate comes from the vertex shader unchanged; in leafMesh it is the terrain normal under the tree (an indexed constant), not the leaf's own | **inferred** not the shading normal: probe dropped |

The diffuse probe coordinate is never reflected, negated or scaled; reflections go to the specular cube only. Snow
variants blend a snow normal into the same register that the sun `dp3` uses, so it is still the shading normal.

**The automatic test** in `generate.cjs`: a probe read whose coordinate is an input register (`v#`, `t#`) that no other
instruction reads has no probe normal. It splits the 48 Counters shadow variants from the 204 raw-input reads that are
normals (those also feed the light), and drops the 102 foliage and particle shaders above. `ps_3_0` is unchanged (4,248);
`ps_2_0` probe normals 2,119 → 1,969 in the table.

## Smoke job

The GB-02 job (`gbuffer-motion-smoke.json`, `-AB`) with two more variants after the six views:

- `ao-normals`: views off, Ambient Occlusion on with `gbufferNormals`. Pass: `[AO] G-buffer: normals=` above 0, `temporal=0`.
- `ao-temporal`: the same with `temporal`. Pass: `[AO] Temporal filter running` and a `G-buffer` line with `temporal=`
  above 0. Fail: `[AO] Temporal filter off`.
- **Read after the run:** albedo view: Sims and the painted ground in their colours (no longer mid grey); `ao-normals`
  against the `on` load's picture: no dashed shade along counter tops and wall seams, no new dark rims; `ao-temporal`
  during the pan: no trails on walls, Sims and trees without smear; `restarts=` small next to `temporal=`.

## Open issues

- No game run in this package.
- The temporal filter has nothing to accumulate while the shade is deterministic; its gain shows once a consumer jitters
  (WP12, GI) or AO lowers its slices with a per-frame rotation.
- Albedo of ghost and translucent Sims, the hair visualiser and the 25 terrain lighting-only passes stays unknown.
- Terrain albedo can exceed 1 (detail × 2) and is clamped by the 8-bit target.
- Foliage keeps no probe normal in `ps_2_0` (class 3 there anyway); a per-pixel leaf normal would need its own rule.
- Draws through Night Lighting's replacement shaders take the game shader's row but not its albedo rule.
