## Deliverable

`server-export.md` section 11: what a patched DXVK would need, its cost against the texel tier, and the verdict.

## Findings

- [V] DXVK 3.1.1 translates shader model 3 through `dxbc_spv::sm3::Converter` (`src/d3d9/d3d9_shader.cpp:1115-1124`,
  submodule `subprojects/dxbc-spirv` at `bf14419`), so a per-pixel ray query means forking two repositories, not one.
- [V] DXVK 3.1.1 enables no ray-tracing extension (also R-01 section 4).
- [I] Per-pixel rays cost 8 M (1080p) to 33 M (4K) rays a frame for 4 lamps, every frame; R-02's texel tier costs
  0 rays a frame once settled, rays only on build changes and lamp moves.
- **Verdict: no-go now.** Reopen conditions are in section 11.3 (visible texel-shadow failure on Sims after capsule
  occlusion, worker headroom in R-G1, upstream ray query in DXVK or a converter extension point).
