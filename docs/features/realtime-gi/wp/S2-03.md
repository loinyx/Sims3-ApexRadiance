# S2-03: the wall-plan field on the GPU

**Status:** partial. The passes and the trace are ported and proven offline against the CPU form; they are not yet
called from Apex (the gather of S2-02 does not exist on this base). Branch `gi/s2-03`, on top of `gi/s0-06`.
Unblocks S2-02 (the trace function for its gather) and X-05 (the field as a texture).

## What exists

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `shaders/plan_field_hlsl.h` | `FloodPS` (one jump flooding pass), `DistPS` (seed to distance, one band a channel), `PlanTrace` (the sphere trace, a function for the gather), `TracePS` (the harness's pass over point pairs) |

## The passes

| Pass | Reads | Writes | Slots (compiler's count, ps_3_0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seeds (CPU, `SeedBand`) | wall edges | `G32R32F`, w x h, uploaded a band | - |
| `FloodPS`, 1 + log2 + 1 times a band | seeds, 9 point reads | `G32R32F` target, two swapped | 40 (2 texture, 38 arithmetic), a loop over the 9 reads |
| `DistPS`, once a band | seeds, 1 read | one channel of the field (colour write mask): band 0 red, 1 green, 2 blue, a fourth alpha | 14 (2 texture, 12 arithmetic) |
| `PlanTrace` in `TracePS`, 4 point reads a step | field | - | 113 (12 texture, 101 arithmetic), a loop; the same for 16, 32 or 128 steps |
| `PlanTrace`, filtered read (1 a step) | field, bilinear | - | 91 (6 texture, 85 arithmetic) |

The counts hold one pass of each loop; the budget is the loop's integer constant. All fit the 512 slots every ps_3_0
device has. A trace of 32 steps runs about 2 500 instructions at most, the flood about 350.

The field is one `A16B16G16R16F` texture a story: up to four bands in its channels, so a step reads one texel
position whatever band the segment is in. Sizes, w = tiles x px + 4:

| Story | px a tile | Field size | Field, half float | Seeds, each of 2 targets and the upload |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 x 8 tiles (the cases) | 32 | 388 x 260 | 0.8 MB | 0.8 MB |
| 30 x 40 tiles | 32 | 964 x 1284 | 9.9 MB | 9.9 MB |
| 64 x 64 tiles | 16 | 1028 x 1028 | 8.5 MB | 8.5 MB |
| 64 x 64 tiles | 32 | 2052 x 2052 | 33.7 MB | 33.7 MB |

A 64 x 64 story at 32 px takes 15 passes a band (14 floods and the distance), 45 for the three bands, on a build edit
of that story only.

## Findings

- **Verified:** the flood's seeds come out the same as the CPU's (the float field differs only in the square root's
  last bits), and the trace on the float field gives the CPU's verdict on every pair.
- **Verified:** a half-float field with the sampler's bilinear filter is enough: 1 read a step in place of 4, and the
  pairs that change are those that graze a jamb within 1 / 32 px. This is the recommended form for the gather.
- **Verified:** the distance of "no wall" must be finite and under the half-float range: 32 768 px is written, over
  any lot's diagonal; the seed of "no seed" is -1e15, not an infinity.
- **Verified:** the compiler's output for `break` inside an `if` in the loop never left the loop on the device (every
  pair read as blocked). The loop ends through its own condition, which compiles to a working `break_ne`. A state
  kept as -1, 0, 1 and tested with `== 0` was compiled to its absolute value stored back; the trace keeps two
  flags instead.
- **Verified:** Direct3D 9 of Windows reports 4 096 ps_3_0 slots on this GPU and DXVK 32 768; the passes need 113.
- **Inferred:** the cost in the 32-bit address space under the bridge is the upload only. The field and the two seed
  targets are default-pool targets and live in the server; the seeds of one band cross as one texture (the table's
  last column, once in the client while it is filled). Without the bridge the targets take video memory and the
  same upload.
- **Inferred:** a budget of 32 steps with the filtered read is about 32 texture reads a traced pair at most, median
  6 steps (S0-06).

## The server form (`serverCompute`, server-export.md of R-01)

Not built; the same kernel on the server's second device:

- The wall edges of a story cross the bridge with the scene (a few tens of bytes an edge); the server draws the
  seeds itself, so no seed texture crosses.
- Flood: one compute dispatch a pass over two `rg32f` storage images, 8 x 8 groups; one pass a slice keeps a
  dispatch under the 1 ms of R-02 and R-03.
- Distance: into an `rgba16f` image (the `capBits` float16 storage bit), sampled with a linear sampler by the gather.
- Trace: `PlanTrace` as written, with a plain `break`; no slot limit.

## Open issues

- Call the build from Apex on a story's build edit and bind the field in the gather (with S2-02).
- The story walk between stories (`Walk`, `SlabOpen`) is not in the shader: `PlanTrace` is one story. The gather
  either traces each piece or keeps lamps to their story until the stairwell case is ported.
- The seeds are still made on the CPU and uploaded; drawing the wall edges as lines into the seed target would
  remove the upload (33.7 MB a band for the largest lot at 32 px).
- No timings were taken.
- An 8-bit field was not tried; the half-float field is 8 bytes a pixel.
