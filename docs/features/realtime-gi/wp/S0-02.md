## What it does

`run.ps1 -PathRef` builds the harness as before and runs `check.cpp --pathref`, which returns before any
Direct3D 9 device is made (CPU only, all hardware threads).

- Scene: the occluder proxy (`RtLampProxy::Build`), story 0's ground as one quad a tile at `FloorOf`, furniture as
  boxes (`RtLampProxy::AddShape`), the lamp table (`LampDef` through `ToLamp`, the game's law `GpuLampLaw::Eval`,
  colour set to white so the layers are unit colour). Grey Lambertian surfaces, `--albedo` (default 0.5, the constant
  of design §4.3 until S0-12). Black sky.
- Estimator: direct = law x one random point of the lamp ball (0.15 m) per sample; indirect = cosine-weighted paths of
  up to `--bounces` (default 4) vertices with next-event estimation at each vertex. All lamps share each path.
- Texels in the buffer layout of design §3.3: one a tile and story at the tile's centre, five faces: up at floor +
  0.02 m, then +x, -x, +z, -z at floor + 1 m (the directional layers). Only tiles with a floor get a texel.
- Output: with `--out` (run.ps1: `-OutDir\pathref`), one PFM a scene, lamp and story,
  `pathref_<scene>_l<lamp>_s<story>.pfm`. RGB float, width 5 w (faces up, +x, -x, +z, -z), height 2 h (image rows
  top down: h rows of indirect with tile row z = image row, then h rows of direct). Values are in the law's units
  (1.0 = 255 in an 8-bit map, before any clamp).
- Scenes: `rooms` (the harness's two stories of rooms with a door + 5 furniture boxes), `sealed` (the same with the
  door shut), `house` (the synthetic house: balcony, window, door, garden wall, porch roof), and any `-Worlds`
  captures (`LoadWorld`, `-Lamps` a lot).

## Findings

- Indoors at albedo 0.5, the 4th bounce still carries 3.4 % of the light, so stopping at 4 bounces leaves about that
  much out. Stopping at 2 leaves out about 10 % (inferred from the bounce shares; geometric tail). Outdoors the tail
  is under 1 % after 3 bounces. Use `--bounces 8` for a tighter reference.
- Per lamp, indirect / direct summed over texels: 0.64 (table lamp next door) to 1.77 (wall sconce) indoors; 0.24
  (garden post) to 1.34 (under the balcony) outdoors. Indoors, bounce light is about as strong as the direct light
  at these texels (verified, by the run). How that compares with the game's room maps is S0-17's question.
- Skips in visibility tests leak light (verified in this tracer). Skipping 1 cm at both ends of a shadow segment, as
  `RtLampProxy::Visibility` does, let a ball point within 1 cm under a ceiling light the story above (a ceiling lamp
  5 cm under the ceiling lit story 1 at up to 9/255), and let path vertices next to a wall junction see through the
  wall: 764 leaked values in the sealed scene. The tracer has no skip at either end. It also places each path
  vertex `kEps` back along the ray and `kEps` off the surface, so the vertex stays on the side the ray came from.
  With both changes the count is 0. Whether the 1 cm skips cause visible light through ceilings in the shipped
  reference (`Visibility`, used by the shadow checks) has not been checked (open).
- Harness fix (verified, builds): at 65d2a23, `run.ps1` did not compile `check.cpp`. The include paths for
  `framework/`, `features/`, `patches/` and `shaders/` were missing, and `rt_lamp_gpu.cpp` needs
  `ExtraHooks::MarkVertexInput`/`RestoreVertexInput`. The include paths are added, and `check.cpp` now defines
  no-op stubs for both functions (the harness hooks no device).
