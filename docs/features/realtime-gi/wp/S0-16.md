# S0-16: error of a multiplicative apply behind the game's tone curve

**Status:** done (offline). Branch `gi/s0-16`, commit "tools: real-time GI multiplicative apply error".

Deliverable: [tools/realtime_gi_apply_error](../../../../tools/realtime_gi_apply_error/README.md), tables in its
[results.md](../../../../tools/realtime_gi_apply_error/results.md). Tags: **[V]** computed and self-tested here,
**[V-doc]** taken from the repository's verified notes and shipped code, **[I]** inferred.

## Findings

`s` is the relative light change a layer applies (`colour *= 1 + s`): `s = k dE / (E + Emin)` for bounce detail,
`s = -w x occlusion` for contact, capsule and rig shadows. Errors are 8-bit levels of the finished pixel.

1. **Walls, objects and Sims: no curve error.** Their shaders are linear in the map light [V-doc: the tanh curve is in
   the 6 interior-floor techniques only, 104 pixel shaders]. The apply is exact there up to rounding (at most
   `0.5 (1 + s)` level from scaling an 8-bit pixel) [V].
2. **Floors: the curve acts on light x albedo, so the error depends on the pixel alone** [V-doc for the curve's place,
   V for the consequence]. The true pixel is `c (1 + s) tanh(atanh(u) q / u) / q`, `u = mean(c)`,
   `q = mean(c (1 + s))`. The curve's constant cancels, so the unresolved base of the shader's `exp` does not matter.
3. **The plain apply always overshoots on floors:** too bright when brightening, too dark when darkening. For a small
   term the relative error is `s (1 - atanh(u) (1 - u^2) / u)`: 0.7% of `s` at pixel mean 26, 6% at 76, 18% at 128,
   37% at 178, 51% at 204, 69% at 230 [V].
4. **Error bound, small terms (`|s| <= 0.1`)** [V]: at most 1 level on floor pixels up to mean 94, 2 levels up to 118,
   4 levels up to 148; worst case 16 levels on the brightest possible floor pixel (229).
5. **Error bound, large terms** [V]: `s = -0.5` (a contact shadow taking half the light): 1 / 2 / 4 levels up to pixel
   mean 79 / 98 / 122, 15 levels at 178, 44 at 229. `s = +0.5`: 1 / 2 / 4 levels up to 47 / 60 / 75, worst 42.
   `s = +1`: worst 51 levels at mid grey. A full shadow (`s = -1`) is exact.
6. **Coloured terms keep their hue but lose strength the same way** (table 3): a red-only `+0.3` on a mid-grey floor
   is 5 levels too bright in red and 4 in the other two channels [V].
7. **Rooms the game still solves add the solve curve** (maps written by the game, not by the linear compose) [V for
   the numbers, I for where it applies]: on walls 0.3% / 1.2% / 2.7% / 5.2% of the pixel for `s = +0.1` at a map mean
   of 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.6 / 0.8; on floors the two curves compound (table 4, up to +31% for `s = +0.5`).
8. **Frames** [V for the numbers, I as a bound for night interiors]: 26 gameplay frames of a lit kitchen walk
   (JPEG, 1.86 M sampled pixels), every pixel taken as a floor: for `|s| <= 0.1` 97% of pixels stay under 1 level and
   the 99th percentile is 3.3 levels; for `s = -0.5` the 99th percentile is 6 levels, for `s = +0.5` 23 levels.
   No capture with a lamp toggled was available, so the "known added light" is the model's `s`, not a measured pair.

## What it means for the layers

- **Contact, capsule and rig shadows (S5-01 and its siblings):** fine as designed on walls, objects and Sims. On
  bright floors a plain apply darkens visibly too much (finding 5). Use the exact form on floor pixels:
  `P = pow((1 + u) / (1 - u), q / u)`, `colour = colour (1 + s) (P - 1) / ((P + 1) q)`; one `pow`, about 10
  instructions, no constant needed [V for the form, I for the cost].
- **It needs a floor mask.** Applied to a wall pixel the exact form under-applies by the same amount. If the pass
  cannot tell floors [I: the AO receiver classes may already do], apply half the correction: the worst case halves on
  both surface kinds.
- **Bounce detail:** `|s|` is small by design (zero-mean, short range). Cap it at 0.25 and the uncorrected error stays
  under 4 levels up to pixel mean 104; with the floor form there is no cap from the curve.
- **Dark pixels:** scaling an 8-bit pixel multiplies its rounding step by `1 + s`; keep `1 + s <= 2` so banding stays
  within one level [I]. `Emin` must be at least the ambient share of the pixel, or `s` is overstated in dark corners [I].
- **Clipped maps:** where an 8-bit map sits at 1 the true response to more light is zero, so a positive term is wrong
  by its full size there; fade positive terms out as the map nears 1, or use the float buffers [I].
