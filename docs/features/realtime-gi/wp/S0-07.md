# S0-07: room id a tile and wall-aware upsample weights

- Status: done (synthetic stories); the check on captured lots is open
- Branch: `gi/s0-07`
- Deliverable: `features/gi_rooms.h` (pure header), `tools/gi_rooms_test/` (`check.cpp`, `run.ps1`)
- Acceptance: `tools/gi_rooms_test/run.ps1` passes: 12800 sample points over a synthetic story with a dividing wall, a
  diagonal split tile and a free-standing wall inside one room. Every tap is of the point's room, no tap is seen
  through any wall edge (brute force over every edge), weights sum to 1, no point is left without a texel, the
  interior is plain bilinear, and a layer lit only in one room gives exactly 0 on the other side of the wall.

## Findings

- **Room ids come from the game, not from the wall graph** (verified in source): `LevelLightShare`'s story grid
  already reads the room of every tile quadrant from the lighting tile (`TileRoom`, tile `+0x7C + q x 0x14`,
  `FUN_006a9760`; 0 = outside) into `StoryGrid::quad`, and the proxy build copies it to `RtLampProxy::Story::room`
  (`level_light_share.cpp`, the proxy's story loop). No flood fill is needed. This closes open question 3 of research
  page 04 (§3). Matching the game's rooms is by construction; it was not checked on a capture here.
- **Texel rule** (decision): one texel a tile and story takes the room most of its quadrants belong to (ties: the
  lower quadrant index), its point is the centroid of those quadrants. A tile with no floor and no placed quadrant,
  or with only negative ids, has no texel. Room 0 (outside) keeps texels.
- **Upsample rule** (decision): bilinear over the four nearest tile centres; a tap counts only when its texel is of
  the point's room and the segment from the point to the texel's point crosses no wall edge of the story (any edge,
  open or not); weights renormalised. With no bilinear tap left (the minority side of a diagonal split), the nearest
  such texel of the 3 x 3 tiles around the point takes weight 1; otherwise the point gets no indirect light.
- The wall test also holds where rooms do not differ: a free-standing wall inside one room (or outdoors) stops the
  blend (verified on the synthetic story).
- Inferred: a minority half of a diagonal split tile shares its neighbours' texels, so a small half-tile room reads
  the texel of the larger room's tile only when it owns the tile's majority. A per-quadrant texel would fix it at 4x
  memory; not needed unless a capture shows it.
