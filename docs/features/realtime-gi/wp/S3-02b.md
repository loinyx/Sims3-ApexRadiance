## Findings

- **[V]** `tools/lamp_index_test/run.ps1 -Precomp <Shaders_Win32.precomp>`: 67 checks, 0 failures. The mod builds
  (Release x86) without warnings.
- **[V]** Transport, CPU model, both laws: 0 of 38,400 pixels over 1/255 on open ground (objects anywhere) and with
  walls (objects at cell centres, loose, in a wall, gates). Reported arms as in S3-02 (walls anywhere 4,282 over,
  tall objects 19,202, a dense cluster 411).
- **[V]** Slices: after 4 lamps changed (moved, removed, added, faded) only the cells within their reach were picked
  again (3,340 of 4,096 in this 110 m sample) and the result equals a new build cell for cell (rows resolved, not column
  numbers). A window moved by 16 m x 32 m picks 1,408 new cells and keeps the rest; equal to a new build.
- **[V]** CPU time of a whole build with the model pick (4,096 cells, 8,192 picks): 24 ms with 40 lamps, 71 ms with
  150 lamps (17 us a cell) on a quiet machine; 125 ms and 150 ms in a second run on a loaded one. **[I]** The game's pick also tests walls and slabs, so a game build costs more; at 1 ms a
  frame a whole build takes about 25 to 70 frames or more. The in-game line `Lamp index: cells built (...)` logs the
  picks, frames, the total and the worst frame.
- **[V]** The 2 refused object shaders (106 slots, 7 temporaries, highest constant c21) defined `i0` themselves. The
  loop now takes the lowest integer register the host neither defines nor reads: 814 of 814 object shaders take the
  index copy under each law, native D3D9 accepts all, and 624 drawn are equal to the per-draw copy within 1 LSB under
  both laws (622 before the fix).
- **[V]** Fences and stairs: 1,231 hosts, every index copy taken under both laws with and without the floor-tile
  shadows, accepted, 230 drawn equal within 1 LSB. Under the reach law the sample needs the walls' larger lamp
  scale (6 instead of 0.8): at 0.8 the 1/d^2 lamp light stayed under the atlas term of the max and the pictures did not
  depend on the lamps.
- **[V]** Window frames: 14 hosts, every index copy taken under both laws, 7 drawn equal within 1 LSB (a sample
  without cones and with a lamp shining out of a house, which the window copies leave out).
- **[I]** Compare classes (`lampIndex 1`): a per-draw pick that differs from its cell is counted as `cellFull` (no
  slot left), `unexplained` (a new pick at the cell's own point differs from the cell: stale or a fault, must stay 0),
  `group` (fences, not drawn from the cells yet), `offCentre` (the pick at the cell's centre at the object's height
  equals the cell) or `height`.
