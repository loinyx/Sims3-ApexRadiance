## Layout

One `A32B32G32R32F` texture, 576 x 68 (627 KB), point sampled, one sampler.

- **Cells:** a world-space window of 64 x 64 cells of 2 m (128 m), snapped to 16 m around the camera. A cell is 9
  texels in a row: 8 entries `(lamp index, visibility, visibility for an object set in a wall, 0)` and one texel
  `(ground share, highest lamp head, ground share set in a wall, 0)`. Index 0 is a black lamp, so an empty entry needs
  no branch.
- **Lamp table:** rows 64-67, one column a lamp (255 at most): `(position, 1/R^2)`, `(colour, spill)` and the two
  cone rows, the four rows the per-draw fix uploads for a lamp.
- **Frame constants**, pixel shader `c208`-`c214` of the reserved `c208`-`c223` (research 13): cell scale and window
  origin, texel steps, lamp strength and the strength of a gate, the ground atlas uv, ground strength with the lot's
  ground height and the pool gate's two terms, the fade over the highest lamp head.
- **Per draw:** one row, `(set in a wall, gate in a fence gap, 0, 0)`. The first selects the second visibility and
  share, the second the gate's lamp strength.

A cell holds what `PickObjectLamps` answers at its centre at the lot's ground height, asked twice (loose: walls
nearer than 0.2 m ignored; set in a wall: 0.75 m), less the lamps whose radius ends before the cell. The shader takes
the cell from the pixel's world position, loops over the 8 entries (`rep`, `texldl`) and takes the ground share and
both height gates at the pixel.

## Findings

- **[V]** The index block is exact when the cell's pick equals the object's: on open ground everywhere, with walls at
  cell centres. It is the transport of the old lighting, not a new look.
- **[V]** `lrp` may not write its first source's register, also across components (X5766). The token block avoids it.
- **[V]** The block costs 10 temporaries, not 16: the HLSL form's count is the compiler's, the token block reuses
  registers. No host comes near the limit.
- **[I]** Two differences from the per-draw fix are by design and visible: visibility and ground share change at cell
  borders (2 m) instead of per object, and elevated pixels take the height gates at their own height.
- **[I]** The cells cost 8,192 picks a build. A build runs when the lamp list or the wall state changes or the camera
  leaves the inner window. Its time is logged (`Lamp index: cells built in ... ms`) and not measured yet.

## Open issues

- **[O]** No game run. `lamp_index` 1 (check) and 2 (draw) are untested in game and on the bridge: the looped
  `texldl` shader, the float texture and constants `c208`-`c214` through the bridge, and the build time.
- **[O]** Realistic reach: the index copy is refused with the reach law, so with realistic reach on every object
  keeps the pick. The table needs a fifth row (`r0^2`) and the reach radius for the reach of a cell.
- **[O]** Fences and stairs (`PatchInstancedLamps`) and the window-frame copy (`PatchWindowOutdoor`) are not ported.
  The cells already carry what they need except the floor-tile shadow rectangle. X-6D step 3.
- **[O]** One ground height a window: the cells are picked at the ground height of the first object's lot, and an
  object on a lot with another height keeps the pick. Stories (a balcony) need cell layers.
- **[O]** The window follows the camera eye, not the view target: far zoomed out, objects fall outside and keep the
  pick. Both fallbacks are counted (`kept the pick` in the shutdown line).
- **[O]** The build is not sliced over frames. **[I]** A lamp whose fade changes starts a new lamp generation at every lamp read (every 20 frames), so a fading lamp rebuilds the cells that often.
- **[O]** The switch is a development switch. It should become `[patches.RealtimeGI] lampIndex` and the constants
  move to the frame block of S3-01 when both exist.
- **[O]** The 2 refused shaders were not looked at (no room, or an integer constant of their own).
