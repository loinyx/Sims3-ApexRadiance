Sim materials (`kSimReceiverPs`, the list Ambient Occlusion's Sim receivers already use in game) and the scene's depth
is bound.

## Mesh casters without Sims (MC-2 open issue)

MC-2 put the mesh instances in as static occluders (custom index 0, mask 1). In the delta form a static hit only
removes a lamp from the multiplier, so a mesh never darkened anything, and the kernel traced only inside a capsule's
reach. Now:

- Mesh instances are delta casters, as research 16 §2 counts furniture detail. Where the texel tier's static boxes
  (custom index 1) are sent, a mesh inside its box adds nothing under the box's shadow; today Apex sends no boxes.
- The server groups the instances' world bounds into at most 32 boxes after the block's casters; the kernel's reach
  test reads them. A lot without Sims traces its furniture.
- [V] `test-gi-mesh.cmd` mode `on`: see Checks.
- [I] Under a furniture shadow the texel layers already darken, the mesh darkens again (XR-07's open "double
  darkening", XR-10).
- Part (2) of the package, interim furniture boxes, is dropped: MC-2's meshes replace them. Objects whose LITE does
  not resolve (MC-2 test: 1 of 2) cast nothing; boxes for those wait for the in-game miss share (`[GiMesh] missingShare=`).

  Sim draws that costs in game.

  capsule fit to the bones needs the rig's bind joints (S5-05 or XR-10).
- [I] The shadow-map and reflection filter is the rigid-matrix test plus the scene depth; a pass with this camera's
  projection from elsewhere would pass.

- [O] Mesh casters with no texel box under them add their whole shadow; with boxes in the block they add only the detail.
