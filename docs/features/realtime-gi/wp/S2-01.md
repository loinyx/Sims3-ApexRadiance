## What it does

`GiSched::Scheduler` is pure logic (no game reads, no device). Per lamp of the active lot it keeps the layer state: the
touch box of the shown layer (tiles, stories), the dirty cause, the phase (idle, waiting to stand still, queued, in
flight, idle list), the crossfade. The rules are those of [X-REBAKE](X-REBAKE.md) "Scheduler":

| Event (S1-02 `Changes()` or build) | What happens |
|---|---|
| switched, value (dim, colour) | No solve. The tiles of the lamp's box go to this frame's compose list (`composeOnly`) |
| moved, added | Waits until the lamp stood still 2 frames, then queued. A move while its bake is in flight cancels the bake (`restarts`): once, not once an edit |
| removed | Bake cancelled, layer dropped from the tile lists, its tiles composed |
| build edit (geometry key of the lot changed) | Collected; once edits stopped for 0.5 s the scene is sent, then every lamp whose touch box holds the edit re-bakes. Nothing is issued while edits go on |
| active lot changed (or the first in a world) | Every layer dropped, the scene sent, every lamp queued as `load` |

- **Priority:** an entry queued 180 frames ago goes first (no starvation), then lamps on before lamps off, in view
  (lamp point in the camera's clip box), camera distance in 2 m steps, cause (moved, added, build, load), age.
- **Budget:** 2 bakes issued a frame, 8 in flight (`GiSched::Budget`); `overBudget` must be 0.
- **Cap:** 64 queued; the rest wait in an idle list that refills the queue once the queue and the bakes in flight are
  empty. A failed bake goes to the idle list (retried once the queue drains, not every frame).
- **Per-tile compose list:** `TileLamps(tile)` gives the lamps whose layer touches a tile; a crossfading lamp is in the
  tiles of both slots. `ComposeTiles()` is the frame's list of tiles to compose again.
- **Crossfade:** a landed layer goes to the second slot and fades in over 0.2 s (`Blend(lamp)`); the stale layer stays
  shown until then; a layer landing during a fade takes the faded one as shown first (a layer is replaced whole).

In game (`realtime_gi.cpp`, render thread, after the snapshot): lamps of the active lot only; build edits from
`LevelLightShare::LotGeometryKey` of the lot's story-0 room 0, read every 10 frames; camera from
`LotLightingMotion::CameraEye` and `PostScene::RecentCameraViewProj`. It runs when the back end in use is a server one,
or with the developer key `[patches.RealtimeGI] debugFakeSolve = true` on the fake back end (a layer lands 3 frames
after its bake starts, with the lamp's reach up to 6 m as its box).

**Counters** (developer mode, every 10 s):
`[GiSched] lot=<id> queued= inFlight= idle= fading= issued: moved= added= build= load=; landed= failed= composeOnly=
tilesComposed= restarts= dropped= sceneSubmits= removed= latency=<p50>/<max> frames maxInFlight=; must be 0: overBudget=`.

**Bridge back end** (`BridgeBackend`): resolves `RemixBridge_GiOpen`, `GiSubmit`, `GiSolve`, `GiPoll`, `GiCancel`;
opens without waiting (`S_FALSE` until the worker has its device); sends the scene in 448 KB parts with `GiSubmitMore`
and retries on `D3DERR_WASSTILLDRAWING` next frame; keeps the scene ticket once it polls `S_OK`; keeps a lamp table
(lamp i = layer i, unit colour so switch, dim and colour stay in the compose, R-04 re-solves a lamp whose 56 bytes
change) and sends it before a solve when it changed; solves the indirect layer (`layer = index`, `GiLayerIndirect`).
The `GiLamp` wire layout (56 bytes) is mirrored and `static_assert`ed.

## Acceptance

- Offline (`tools/gi_sched_test/run.ps1`): all checks pass [V]:

| Sequence | Result |
|---|---|
| Lot load, 100 lamps in a row of rooms | 2 issued the first frame, the camera-near lamps first; 36 over the cap to the idle list; all 100 land; at most 2 issued a frame and 8 in flight; dirty to landed p50 28, max 55 frames |
| Off lamp, view | The nearest lamp, off, bakes after the lamps on; a lamp in view bakes before a nearer one out of view |
| Toggle storm: 600 switches, dims and recolours over 300 frames | 0 solves, 600 compose-only updates; one switch composes exactly its layer's 50 tiles; a tile in two boxes lists both lamps |
| Drag: 40 frames of moves | 0 bakes during the drag; the bake starts 2 frames after the last move; one restart and one cancel when moved again in flight; one layer lands; latency counts from the first move. Added: waits, bakes once. Removed: layer dropped, 50 tiles composed |
| Wall edit burst: an edit every 100 ms for 2 s | Nothing issued and no scene during the burst; the scene within 2 frames after 500 ms of no edits, once; then exactly the 2 lamps whose box holds the wall. A whole-lot edit re-bakes all 20 |
| Crossfade | 12 frames at 60 fps (200 ms), blend never decreasing, the tiles composed every frame of it; the stale layer stays during the re-bake; both slots in the tile lists while fading, the old one gone after |
| Starvation: 1 bake a frame, 1 in flight, 20-frame bakes, 10 near lamps moved every 3 frames | The far lamp lands within the 180-frame bound plus the in-flight bakes. A failed bake is retried from the idle list and lands |

## Findings

- [V] (code) S1-02's snapshot commits one change kind a lamp a commit (switch wins over move and value), so a
  switch never reaches the scheduler as a move.
- [V] (code) `OutdoorDelta::LotLamps`, the active-lot source of S1-02, is filled only while the GPU room lamps capture
  runs (S1-02 Findings). With it off the active lot stays 0 and the scheduler does nothing: the smoke job then fails
  its `lot=` pattern instead of passing hollow.
- [I] `LotGeometryKey` changes on lamp-free game edits too (any wall top, cut or room-0 blocker on the lot, including
  objects as 3D blockers): without an edit box every such change re-bakes all lamps with a layer.
- [V] (R-04 wp) a reflectance-only change needs a full scene submit (links traced again); the scheduler treats it as a
  build edit.

## Open issues

- **Compose pass and layer textures** (next package): the two DEFAULT-pool slots a layer (`BridgeBackend::target`),
  the compose of `ComposeTiles()` with `Blend()`, and the colour scale a lamp (the table colour is unit).
- **Scene blob builder:** lot proxy (`ExportLotProxy`) to `GiScene` triangles, patches (one a wall line and side over
  all stories, R-04 finding), map and texels, in world space as the lamps; until it fills `BridgeBackend::scene`,
  `SubmitScene` refuses and nothing is issued on the server back end.
- **Edit box:** the build edit has no place (whole-lot re-bake). The scene builder diffs the proxy it sends and passes
  the changed box to `OnBuildEdit`.
- **Touch box from the server** (R-04: not built; with R-06's slot). Until then the box is the lamp's reach (6 m at most).
- **Region box and ball radius** in the lamp table: sent as whole scene and 0.1 m (`shortcut:` comments).
- Camera story: the cap keeps the highest priority entries, not the camera's story as X-REBAKE says (no camera-story
  source used here).
- `ServerRayQuery()` reports rays until `GiOpen` answers; the start line can say `serverRays` on a compute-level server.
- Furniture moves (server tiers) have no event source here.
