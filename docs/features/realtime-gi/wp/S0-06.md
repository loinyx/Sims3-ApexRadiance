The field is built on the CPU, but pass for pass as pixel shaders would build it: whole-buffer passes, 9 reads a
pixel, two buffers swapped. S2-03 ports the passes; the trace is the loop of `TraceStory`.

## The method

- **Plan.** 16 px a tile. Each wall edge is sampled four times a pixel; a pixel keeps the wall point nearest its
  centre as its seed (sub-pixel), so the distance of a pixel centre to a straight wall is exact. Diagonal walls need
  no special case.
- **Field.** Jump flooding with one pass at step 1 before and after the usual N/2 .. 1 passes: 10 passes for a
  12 x 8 tile story (196 x 132 px), 13 for a 64 x 64 tile lot. The result is the distance to the nearest seed in
  pixels.
- **Trace.** Bilinear read; blocked under 0.75 px; step = distance - 0.75 px, at least 0.25 px. A wall crosses a
  pixel within 0.71 px of each of the four centres around any of its points, so a continuous wall always reads
  under 0.75 px and the step never passes it. A step budget that runs out counts as blocked.
- **Stories.** The segment is cut at the story planes. Each piece is traced in its story's field; at a plane the
  crossing point must have no ceiling of the lower story (`roofed`) and no placed floor of the upper one
  (`placed`). Stairwells and removed floors pass by that test alone.
- **Twin.** The same walk with exact 2D segment tests against the wall edges, under the same band rule; with no
  bands it tests each wall at the height the segment crosses it.

## The sill-height rule

A story's 3 m are cut into height bands, one plan a band. A wall span is drawn into a band's plan when the wall is
solid at the band's middle height. The trace reads the band the segment is in at each step and never steps past the
end of that band.

Default cuts: **1.0 m and 2.15 m**, three bands with middles at 0.5 m, 1.575 m and 2.575 m:

| Wall part | Under 1.0 m | 1.0 - 2.15 m | Over 2.15 m |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full wall | blocks | blocks | blocks |
| Half wall (top at 1 m, the game's constant `[0x0107A538]`) | blocks | open | open |
| Window (sill over 0.5 m, head over 1.575 m) | blocks | open | blocks |
| Door or arch (sill 0, head under 2.575 m) | open | open | blocks |

Measured on the two-room case with a window (sill 0.9 m, head 2.1 m) and two half-wall tiles, 950 625 point pairs,
the rule against the exact heights (twin with bands against twin without):

| Bands | Passes what the exact test blocks | Blocks what the exact test passes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 25.3 % | 0 % |
| 2, cut at 1.0 m | 7.6 % | 1.9 % |
| 3, cut at 1.0 and 2.15 m (default) | 0.41 % | 1.9 % |
| 4 equal | 7.5 % | 0 % |
| 8 equal | 3.3 % | 3.7 % |

Cuts at the heights where openings begin and end beat more bands at equal spacing. The two-plan form of
research 12 §4.2a (one sill height) is the second row.

## Findings

- **Verified:** a closed wall, straight or diagonal, passes nothing at any step budget.
- **Verified:** the field never opens a doorway wider than half a pixel past its jambs (3 cm at 16 px a tile). It
  narrows it: the blocked radius of 0.75 px takes about 0.5 px off each jamb, which is the 7.4 % of pairs the field
  blocks and the twin passes at a 0.8 m door. At 32 px a tile that falls to 1.6 %, with 0.12 % of blocked pairs
  passed, all within the half-pixel jamb tolerance. The proxy's walls have no thickness and the game's walls do, so
  the narrowing is on the side of the real geometry.
- **Verified:** between stories the plan walk agrees with the proxy mesh except at the
  slab's thickness: the twin passes 2.2 % of the pairs the mesh blocks at the stairwell, by the 0.2 m side of
  the slab around the hole, which the plane test does not see.
- **Verified:** end points must stand off the walls by 1 px or more (0.0625 m at 16 px); the cases use 0.08 m.
  A wall texel's gather point is pushed along its normal by that much before the trace.
- **Verified:** a budget of 16 steps covers 96.7 % of clear traces and 32 steps 99.6 %; the rest read as blocked
  (segments that run along a wall). No leak follows from a short budget.
- **Inferred:** the cuts at 1.0 m and 2.15 m fit the game's doors and windows. Only the half-wall height is a game
  constant; sill and head heights of real lots were not read (no Full capture was at hand).
- **Inferred:** the distance fits an 8-bit or half-float target on the GPU. The field here is 32-bit float; the
  trace needs about a quarter pixel of precision near walls.
