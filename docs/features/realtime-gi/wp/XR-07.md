## A frame

1. Off (`pixelShadows` 0, or Real-Time Lighting off): no export is looked up, no command is sent, nothing is created.
2. On: post-scene effect at `kAmbientOcclusion - 3` (after G-buffer motion, before Ambient Occlusion), in a world, with
   the scene's depth bound. `RemixBridge_GiPixelOpen` each frame until it answers; a state other than on, a missing
   export or a refused open logs `[GiPixel] off: <reason>` once and the feature stays off.
3. Eight `A16B16G16R16F` targets of a quarter of the INTZ size (`width / 4`, as the server checks): raw, guide twice,
   history twice, the a-trous pair, motion. Released at a device reset and made again after it.
4. Block: inverse view-projection (double, `GBufferMotion::Inverse`), the camera's eye, forward and `zFar` taken from it
   and from `CameraNear` / `CameraDepthA`; the 16 lamps that are on with the most colour over distance squared from the
   camera; `samples` 1, `frame` counting, bias 0.01, ambient 0.05, lamp ball 0.1 m (look constants, XR-10).
5. `RemixBridge_GiPixelPass` with outputs 0 (raw) and 1 (guide of this frame); 2 and 3 are not asked for.
6. `MotionQPS` (new): the quarter motion from the raw output's device depth and D = prevViewProj x inverse(viewProj),
   class 0. Then `TemporalPS`, the three a-trous passes (steps 1, 2, 4 with XR-05's strengths) and `UpsamplePS`, with
   a multiply blend on the scene (`ZERO`, `SRCCOLOR`, rgb only). Reset: no previous camera, a cut or a lamp change.
7. Views: `pixelView` 1 draws the filtered mask, 2 the raw mask (output 0's `r`), opaque, instead of the multiply.
8. Developer mode, every 10 s: `[GiPixel] passes=N rays=N raysLast=N gpuUs=N size=WxH faults=N sent=N refused=N`.

The device state is kept by one `D3DSBT_ALL` state block (as `rt_lamp_gpu.cpp`), the render target and depth by hand.

## Open issues

- [O] No casters: there is no Sim capsule source (XR-08) and no object proxy for the static boxes, so the kernel
  traces no ray. `pixelTestCaster` hangs one capsule 0.4 m beside and under the strongest lamp so the smoke shows rays
  and the view shows a shadow; it is a developer switch only.
- [O] The multiplier is the raw visibility `r` (XR-05's first form). The lamp-weighted form from outputs 2 and 3
  (research 16 §4.1) and the double darkening under furniture belong to XR-10.
- [O] Motion class is 0 until GB-02: foliage and Sims are treated as static by the history.
- [I] Lamps are picked by colour over distance from the camera, not filtered to the camera's lot.
- [O] Cost in game: the counters give `gpuUs` of the pass; the filter passes are untimed.
