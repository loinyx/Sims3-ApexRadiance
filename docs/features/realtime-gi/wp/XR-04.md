# XR-04: the per-pixel delta shadow kernel, standalone

Status: **done** (offline harness). Branch `gi/xr-04` (off `gi/x-rtpp`, with `gi/r-02` merged for the harness).

Marks: [V] verified by the harness on an RTX 3070 Laptop GPU, [I] inferred, [O] open.

## Deliverable

`tools/gi_server_test/pixel_shadow.comp` (the kernel, GLSL compute with ray queries) and `pixel_check.cpp` (scene,
synthetic depth, CPU reference, checks). Run: `tools/gi_server_test/run.ps1 -Pixel` (`-TestArgs --size <w> <h>`,
`--device <text>`). Same pinned build inputs as R-02, no SDK install. `pixel_check.cpp` includes R-02's `check.cpp`
for the lot, the device and the buffer code and does not edit it.

## What the kernel does

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Guide | Quarter resolution. Of each 4x4 block the pixel nearest the camera; world position from the 24-bit depth and the inverse view-projection; normal from the depth of the neighbour whose depth is nearer, per screen axis |
| Lamps | Up to 4 a pixel, the strongest by colour x N.L / distance^2; a lamp under A / 128 is left out |
| Reach | A pixel traces only when the segment to one of its lamps crosses the bounds of a delta caster (grown by the emitter radius). Other pixels cost no ray and output 1 |
| Rays | One a lamp and frame, toward a fixed 16-point set on the emitter ball; frame f takes point (f + pixel hash) mod 16. A lamp with a caster in reach: one traversal over static and delta instances; a lamp without: static only (instance mask) |
| Scene | One top level, four instances: walls and floors (both sides), object boxes (culled), detail meshes (culled), capsule boxes with an analytic capsule test |
| Output | Per lamp slot two counts: samples that passed the static occluders (u) and, of those, samples a delta caster blocked (d); the multiplier of research 16 §4.1 from them: `m = 1 - sum(c_i d_i) / (A n + sum(c_j u_j))` |

## Findings

- [V] Occlusion against the CPU reference traced from the kernel's own guide, with no reach test on the CPU: point
  emitters 100 % of 127 367 pixels equal (worst 0.0/255); emitter balls with 16 samples 99.996 % of the delta counts
  equal, 99.999 % of the multipliers within 2/255 (worst 5.7/255). The reach test lost no shadow.
- [V] No self-shadow. Against the drawn surfaces themselves (exact point and normal), lit pixels the kernel shadows:
  16 of 107 485 on walls and floors, 1 of 9 643 on object boxes, **0 of 1 457 on the chair inside its box**, 1 of 629
  on the Sim inside its capsules. The few are shadow edges moved by the origin's offset.
- [V] Counts add up: 16 frames of one sample equal the 16-sample dispatch on every pixel, and a frame traced again is
  byte-equal. A still picture therefore settles to the exact 16-sample value (the base of XR-05's freeze).
- [V] Rays a frame: 103 756 at 480 x 270 guide pixels = 20 % of the cap of 4 a pixel, from 21.7 % of the pixels;
  never more than 4 a pixel.
- [V] Guide: position within 0.30 mm of the drawn surface (clip planes 0.1 m and 1000 m); the depth normal is within
  8 degrees on 100 % of the pixels inside a surface.
- [V] The self-shadow guard is back-face culling plus an entry-only capsule test, and it needs consistent winding:
  the proxy's object shapes (`RtLampProxy::AddShape`) face inward, so their instances carry
  `TRIANGLE_FRONT_COUNTERCLOCKWISE`; without it 8 % of the static counts were wrong. Walls stay two-sided.
- [I] Storing u and d as counts instead of a visibility makes "a static occluder hides the lamp" exact per sample and
  keeps the accumulation in integers; 8 bits a slot hold up to 255 frames.
- [O] Time: 3.1 ms at best, 10.5 ms median for a frame of 104 k rays (submit to idle, the GPU was shared), far above
  research 16 §5's 0.1-1 ms. Not analysed; candidates: the submit itself, the traversal that confirms no candidate
  (every triangle on the ray is visited), the 64-byte debug output. XR-09 measures on a quiet machine.

## Open issues

- The depth is a buffer of floats and the output carries the guide's position and normal for the checks; XR-06
  samples the depth image and writes the `A8R8G8B8` mask only.
- A floor pixel under the chair lies inside the chair's static box: the box no longer hides the lamp for it, so the
  seat's delta shadow multiplies a texel the box shadow already darkened. Needs the texel layer's value or a rule for
  origins inside a static shape (XR-07 or XR-10).
- Lamps beyond the 4 chosen are missing from the multiplier's denominator; `A` (0.25 here) and the clip planes are
  placeholders. Lamp choice ignores walls, so a lamp behind a wall can take a slot (it costs static rays and adds 0).
- The reach test is per pixel against a list of caster bounds; research 16 §5's 16 x 16 tile list is not built.
- The drawn Sim is its capsules at 0.8 of their radius; a mesh wider than its capsules was not tested.
- Not built here (XR-05): the filter, history, reprojection, the caster cap.

## Unblocks
