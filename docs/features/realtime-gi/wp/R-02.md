# R-02: standalone texel solver on Vulkan

Status: **done for the synthetic lot; partial against the plan's acceptance** (no captured lot and no S0-02 reference
were available, see Open issues). Branch `gi/r-02`.

Marks: [V] verified by the harness on an RTX 3070 Laptop GPU, [I] inferred, [O] open.

## Deliverable

`tools/gi_server_test/` (`run.ps1`, `check.cpp`, `solve.comp`). `run.ps1` builds a 64-bit test program and runs it.
Build inputs are fetched once into the output folder at pinned versions; nothing is installed and no Vulkan SDK is
needed: Vulkan headers and the volk loader (`vulkan-sdk-1.4.350.1`), glslang `16.6.0` (compiles `solve.comp` into
five SPIR-V headers at build time).

## What it does

| Step | Rays | Per |
|---|---|---|
| Scene | The occluder proxy of `features/rt_lamp_proxy.h` for a two-story lot: lamp room A (window, two object boxes), room C joined to A by a door, closed room B, closed room upstairs; floors added by the harness. One bottom-level and one top-level acceleration structure | Build change |
| Links | Cosine-weighted rays a texel; each stores the texel it hits (the light buffer is read at the hit, no material on the server). Fixed ray set a texel | Build change, **lamp independent** |
| Direct | 16 shadow rays to points on the lamp's emitter ball | Lamp |
| Bounces | A gather over the links, previous indirect as emitter, repeated until no texel moves more than 0.1/255. **No ray** | Lamp |

One shader source serves both back ends: `RAYQUERY` uses `GL_EXT_ray_query`; without it the same kernel walks the
proxy's 1 m grid (`RtLampProxy::BuildGrid`) in a Vulkan 1.1 compute shader. Each lamp has its own layer.

## Findings

- [V] **No leak, exactly 0.** A lamp in A adds 0 (direct and indirect, bit-exact) to every texel of closed room B and
  of the closed room upstairs; a lamp in B adds 0 to A, C and upstairs. Both back ends. The door room C is lit
  (direct through the door on about 12% of its texels, indirect on all).
- [V] Direct light equals the CPU reference (`RtLampProxy::Clear`, same emitter points): 100% of texels within 2/255,
  worst 0.0/255, three lamps, both back ends.
- [V] Multi-bounce energy balance of the closed room (absorbed = emitted): 1.008 at 4 texels a metre and 256 rays,
  1.010 at 8 texels a metre and 64 rays.
- [V] Back-end parity: 1 to 4 of 2.3 M links differ; 100% of texels within 2/255 (worst 1.1/255); mean ratio 1.0000.
- [V] A second solve is byte-equal (fixed ray set, so a settled layer carries no noise in time).
- [V] The compute back end runs on a device with no ray-tracing extension enabled, and on an integrated GPU
  (`--no-rays --device Intel`), with all checks passing.
- [V] Bounces to settle: 13 to 17 a lamp with albedo 0.3 to 0.8. Design 3.5's "2-4 bounces" is not enough for the
  0.1/255 criterion in a bright closed room; since a bounce is a gather with no ray, the count is cheap.
- [I] **Design consequence:** rays are needed only for the links (a build change) and the shadow rays (a lamp move).
  Switching or recolouring costs nothing, a moved lamp costs its shadow rays plus gathers. Link memory is
  texels x rays x 4 bytes: 40 k texels x 64 rays is 10 MB of server memory.
- [V] Solve buffers must live in device memory: with host-visible buffers the bounce passes ran about 30 times slower.
- [O] **Timing is not settled.** The machine was shared with other GPU work during the runs, and the printed numbers
  varied by more than 10 times between runs (links of 2.3 M rays: 1 to 60 ms with ray queries, 130 to 210 ms in
  software; acceleration structures of 1 256 triangles: 4 to 180 ms). Order of magnitude only: a whole 9 k-texel
  lot with three lamps settled in 0.3 to 2.6 s including a read-back a bounce. A clean run belongs to R-G1.

## Open issues

- Captured lots: the `World.txt` reader lives inside `tools/rt_lamps_test/check.cpp` and was not moved to a shared
  header (other packages edit that file). The harness therefore runs the synthetic lot only.
- "Matches S0-02 within the look band" is not checked: S0-02 and S0-01 outputs did not exist. The CPU direct
  reference, the energy balance and the parity of two independent tracers stand in for it.
- The hit-to-texel lookup is a world-cell map that assumes 3 m stories and walls on tile lines (marked `shortcut:` in
  `check.cpp`). R-04 needs a texel index a proxy triangle (or patch) for captured lots, split levels and diagonals.
- Object boxes block light and return none (no texels on furniture); ground outside holds no texel.
- Not built: the edge-aware texel filter of design 3.9, directional layers, the room ambient term, compose and look
  constants, wall-aware upsample, a picture dump. At 64 rays the layers show per-texel sampling noise in space.
- Convergence is tested on the CPU after a read-back a bounce; the server should test it on the device or run a
  fixed count.

## Unblocks

parity checks), S0-02 comparison once its maps exist.
