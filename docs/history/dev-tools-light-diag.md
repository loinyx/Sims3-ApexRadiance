# Light Diag: history

Chronological record of investigations and design decisions. Newest entries at the bottom. The current behaviour is
described in [features/dev-tools/light-diag.md](../features/dev-tools/light-diag.md).

### 2026-09-24: first snapshots

**Context:** the combined build wrote the dump to `Documents\Electronic Arts\<localized game folder>\S3SS\S3SS_LightDiag.txt`
(`ConfigPaths::GetS3SSDirectory()`), truncated each time, on Ctrl+Shift+F8 or a button under Apex tab > Night Lighting
> Developer > Tools ("Save light diagnostic" + "(or Ctrl+Shift+F8)"). The hotkey poll and the button existed only when
`!kPublicBuild`; `Init` also ran in the public build, harmlessly. Status read `Saved S3SS_LightDiag.txt: N lights, M
lot stories, R rooms`; a missing world wrote `Nenhum mundo carregado.` to the file. Archived copy: `S3SS_LightDiag-1.txt`.

**Finding:** enclosed plaza rooms (walls without a roof) have 0 lights on non-active lots (NOTAS 4c, 6). In the lake
test, street lamps have 2 lights at the same spot (+0x130 = 97 and 40), intensity 1.0, colour (1, 0.75, 0.79); the
visual radius must not come from the bounds (+0x134) (NOTAS 4d). "High quality on all lots" was tried as a fix for the
lot-edge cut and also dropped the frame rate to 63 (NOTAS section 1).

**Outcome:** the high-quality switch was rejected. Its leftover lives in the untracked `patches/light_diag_patch.cpp`.

### 2026-09-25: lamp colour and story lists

**Context:** `S3SS_LightDiag-neve-praca.txt` (01:40, with probe capture m17) and an F8 at 01:39; F8 at 09:50 on lot
C49C001BCF2DEA20, levels 0..4.

**Finding:** 56 street lamps (type 11) and 46 lot lamps (type 3) were still pink after the creation-time colour fix:
the object script sets the colour later through `FUN_006B0B50 -> FUN_006BC3E0`. In room 0 of story 0 the lot's own
outdoor lamps appear 2x (type 3) or 3x (type 11); other stories only see type 11 once (through the world cells);
ground-floor type-3 lamps never reach other stories. At 09:50 the same 19 lights were on every story with correct
weights, so the remaining difference was wall blocking, not the list. The notes (about 02:30) called flag 0x04
"outdoor"; the code labels it "room known". Which is right is unverified.

**Outcome:** colour fix at 0x6B0BDE ([lamp-colour.md](../features/night-lighting/lamp-colour.md)); Level Light Share
was built to carry lamps to other stories.

### 2026-09-25: two writers for the same shortcut

**Context:** at about 10:43 an F8 came back without the stories section.

**Finding:** the hotkey is handled by `light_diag.cpp`, while the section had been added to
`patches/light_diag_patch.cpp`. That file is untracked and not in the vcxproj: it registers its own patch "Light
Diagnostics" with its own Ctrl+Shift+F8 poll, status `Pronto`, an extra "all lots high quality" switch calling
`FUN_006A5EF0` (`thiscall(manager, char hq) ret 4`, bytes `8A 44 24 04 56 8B F1 38 86 88 02 00 00`), and no "LUZ POR
PIXEL" section. Its `APEX_REGISTER_FEATURE` is inside `#ifndef S3SS_PUBLIC`. The same applies to the other untracked
leftovers: `patches/call_trace_patch.cpp` (Call Trace, Ctrl+Shift+F10, detours about 1,400 lighting and terrain
functions listed in `trace_targets.h`, generated by `re/scripts/SafeHookList.java`, output `S3SS_CallTrace.txt`),
`patches/lot_edge_lighting_patch.cpp` and `trace_targets.h` (PLANO-SEPARACAO.md: "Untracked and NOT in the vcxproj").

**Outcome:** the section was put in both files at the time; `light_diag.cpp` is the only writer. The leftovers must
never be committed or added to the project.

### 2026-09-25: PASSO3 snapshots

**Context:** PASSO3 increment 0 ("diag only, no visual change"; `PASSO3-PLANO.md` sections 3.7, 4 and 6) added the
"LUZ POR PIXEL" section at about 15:15. Archives: `-passo3.txt` (11:55), `-passo3-holofote.txt` (14:01),
`-passo3-inc0.txt` (16:09).

**Finding:** 11:55: 5,724 lights, 30 lot stories, 92 rooms. 14:01: the test spotlight is one of 5 type-4 lights (vt
0xFF4570), range 30, colour (0.85, 0.86, 1.0), y 63.5. 16:09: k1 = 1, k2 = 0.075, Cmax = 2, street factor 3.3333,
type-5 scale 5, blur passes 2, mode 0, as PASSO3 expected; terrain countdowns -1 (idle); test spotlights axis about
(0, -1, 0), offset -0.32, scale 4.9 (NOTAS 16:25). PASSO3 F-J5: `#5494` was a type-4 spot in one dump and a type-7
window light in the next, so indices are not stable. PASSO3 F-J1 (`passo3\out\fn_0069f650.c`) located the blur
globals; F-J2 the row-vector lot matrix. Test protocol: stay 10 s at the camera, F8, F7 on the wall, F7 on the floor,
send the file and both LightProbe folders.

**Outcome:** kept. Not implemented from PASSO3 3.7: the per-lot key, the lamp block (pointer, W, weight, importance),
self-test errors per type, the last 8 unmatched draw keys, and a shared `LightDiag::ForEachRoom` instead of the two
room walks in `WriteDiag` and `PixelLampDiag`.

### 2026-09-28: story field of lot lamps

**Context:** probe2 capture on a house with a foundation.

**Finding:** the lot's lit lamps were all type 4 with `d0` = 1 or 2.

**Outcome:** led to the terrain bake story gate at 0xC294D9 ([terrain-relight.md](../features/night-lighting/terrain-relight.md)).

### 2026-09-29: story samples only while armed

**Context:** the Level Light Share sample records cost time in every lot light solve.

**Finding:** collecting them unconditionally slowed every solve.

**Outcome:** records are collected only while armed by the Developer checkbox or by the first snapshot of a session.
Sections 1-3 and 5 are unaffected.

### 2026-09-30: player capture in its own folder

**Context:** the Report a problem page was built for players.

**Finding:** a single truncated file lost earlier dumps.

**Outcome:** the snapshot became a player capture in both builds, written to `Captures\<date time> Lighting snapshot\`
and never overwritten ([bug-reports.md](../features/bug-reports.md)). The standalone plan (PLANO-SEPARACAO.md section 4)
had proposed `Apex Radiance\ApexRadiance_LightDiag.txt`; the capture folder replaced it. A background writer working
from a copy of the data, to avoid the hitch, remains an idea only.
